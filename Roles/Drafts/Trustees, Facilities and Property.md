# Trustees, Facilities & Property — Ministry Roles & Responsibilities

**Riverwood Community Church**  
**Status:** Working draft — not adopted  
**Last updated:** November 20, 2026  
**Approval record:** Not yet approved

## Purpose

Trustees help care for Riverwood's building, grounds, equipment and physical resources so the church can provide a safe, welcoming and functional setting for worship, ministries and community activities. They coordinate maintenance, repairs, seasonal preparation and appropriate improvements.

The **Head Trustee** provides overall leadership. Trustees and other facilities volunteers contribute according to their abilities, experience and availability. **Helping with building projects does not require someone to become a Trustee.**

## Leadership and coordination

The Head Trustee is an elected church officer, serves on the Governing Board, and chairs the Trustee Committee. Under the adopted 2024 bylaws, the Head Trustee is elected for a two-year term and forms a committee of **at least four Trustees**, whose names are presented to the Governing Board for confirmation.

The Head Trustee coordinates assignments, building priorities and maintenance projects. Trustees may coordinate work, but purchases, contractor engagements and significant building changes remain subject to established financial and approval authority.

**Paid Custodian:** The Head Trustee is the direct **manager** of the paid Custodian. The Custodian is an employment position, not a volunteer Trustee role. The Custodian's own job description and any employment/service agreement will be maintained **separately**. This document does not set pay, employment terms or independent hiring/termination authority.

## 1. Head Trustee

**Role Type:** Elected church officer  
**Serving Classification:** Church leadership  
**Accountability:** Governing Board  
**Term:** Two years under the adopted bylaws

### Purpose
Lead maintenance, protection and improvement of Riverwood's building, property and physical resources.

### Responsibilities
- Lead the Trustee Committee and recommend Trustee members for Governing Board confirmation.
- Identify, prioritize and coordinate building maintenance, grounds work, repairs and improvement projects.
- Assign work to Trustees and other volunteers based on ability and availability.
- Coordinate professional contractors or service providers where needed and properly authorized.
- Oversee seasonal preparation and responses to significant building problems or emergencies.
- Coordinate records of substantial repairs, major projects, damage and property-related expenditures.
- Help prepare building and property budget recommendations and communicate material concerns to the Governing Board.
- Serve on the Governing Board and Finance Committee as provided in the bylaws.
- **Directly manage the paid Custodian**, including coordinating cleaning priorities and schedules, providing direction and feedback, and addressing routine performance concerns.
- Refer significant employment decisions or changes to compensation, position terms or contractual obligations to the church leadership or body authorized to decide them.
- Work with ministry leaders so facility activities support church programs.

### Decision-Making Authority
Coordinates routine maintenance, Trustee assignments and authorized expenses. The bylaws permit the Head Trustee to sign checks and/or receive a church credit card for **authorized expenses**; this does not grant unlimited spending authority. Major alterations, expenditures or contracts require applicable approvals. Employment decisions beyond routine custodial supervision must follow the church's approved employment authority.

### Service Commitment
Ongoing coordination and availability for significant facility needs, plus Governing Board and related committee duties.

## 2. Trustee

**Role Type:** Governing Board-confirmed Trustee Committee member  
**Serving Classification:** Ministry leadership  
**Accountability:** Head Trustee

### Purpose
Help protect and care for Riverwood's property through maintenance, project coordination and responsible stewardship.

### Responsibilities
- Participate in Trustee discussions and help identify maintenance priorities.
- Monitor assigned parts of the building and grounds for needed repairs.
- Complete or coordinate maintenance tasks within personal skill and authorization.
- Support seasonal preparation and appropriate responses to property emergencies.
- Assist with volunteers or contractors on assigned projects.
- Report safety concerns, material needs, progress and completed work to the Head Trustee.
- Help care for tools, equipment and supplies.

### Decision-Making Authority
May coordinate assigned projects and routine work. Obtain appropriate approval for significant spending, contractor commitments or work outside the assigned scope.

### Serving Guidelines
Construction or maintenance experience is useful but not required. Trustees may contribute through planning, communications, organization or technical skills. No Trustee is expected to know every trade.

### Service Commitment
Participation in committee work, projects and occasional urgent tasks according to availability.

## 3. Facilities & Maintenance Volunteer

**Role Type:** Volunteer  
**Serving Classification:** General volunteer  
**Accountability:** Head Trustee or designated Trustee

### Purpose
Help with specific building, grounds and maintenance projects without holding a Trustee office or committee position.

### Responsibilities
- Assist with suitable repairs, upkeep, cleanup, improvements and seasonal projects.
- Accept work matched to personal skills, training, comfort and availability.
- Follow appropriate safety procedures and directions.
- Care for church tools and workspaces and report unexpected problems.
- Communicate progress and availability to the Trustee coordinating the work.

### Serving Guidelines
No general trade qualification is required for basic tasks. Specialized, hazardous or regulated work must be handled by people properly qualified and authorized.

### Service Commitment
Flexible, project by project.

# Facilities Responsibility Areas

These are **areas of work, not separate offices or standing leadership roles**. A Trustee or facilities volunteer may help in several areas. The Head Trustee coordinates assignments and professional assistance.

## 4. Building Maintenance & General Repairs
**Purpose:** Maintain everyday building condition and function.

**Typical work:** Identify problems, assist with minor repairs, replace approved routine hardware or fixtures, help maintain doors and furnishings, and support small improvement projects. Escalate tasks requiring specialized work.

## 5. Grounds & Exterior Maintenance
**Purpose:** Keep grounds and outdoor access areas safe, functional and welcoming.

**Typical work:** Lawn and landscape care, seasonal cleanup, parking and walkway observations, snow and ice preparation, exterior upkeep, and reporting drainage or exterior-lighting concerns. Use specialized equipment only with proper ability and authorization.

## 6. Electrical & Lighting Maintenance
**Purpose:** Identify and coordinate appropriate responses to lighting and electrical needs.

**Typical work:** Report malfunctioning fixtures, assist with authorized routine lighting maintenance, note emergency and exterior-lighting issues, and coordinate professional repairs. Electrical work requiring permits, licenses or specialized safety practices must be performed by qualified personnel.

## 7. Plumbing & Water Systems
**Purpose:** Help protect the building from leaks, malfunctioning fixtures and water damage.

**Typical work:** Identify leaks or drainage issues, support minor authorized maintenance, monitor accessible areas, and assist with approved winter preparation. Use qualified professionals for major or regulated plumbing work.

## 8. Painting, Patching & Interior Improvements
**Purpose:** Maintain interior appearance and usable spaces.

**Typical work:** Minor patching, surface preparation, painting and finishing, protection of furniture and equipment, cleanup, and approved cosmetic improvements. Coordinate significant changes to rooms or finishes with the relevant ministry leaders.

## 9. Cleaning & Facility Upkeep — Volunteer Support
**Purpose:** Support the cleanliness and organization of church spaces through occasional volunteer projects.

**Typical work:** Deep-cleaning days, storage organization, special-event preparation, cleanup after projects and reporting facility concerns.

**Boundary:** Volunteer work does **not** redefine, replace or alter the paid Custodian's assigned employment duties. The Head Trustee manages the paid Custodian separately.

## 10. Networking & Building Technology Infrastructure
**Purpose:** Support the physical technology infrastructure serving church operations and ministries.

**Typical work:** Network cabling, internet and wireless infrastructure, equipment mounting and related building improvements; identifying issues and coordinating with ministry tech teams.

**Boundary:** Physical facilities support does not automatically give Trustees authority to administer networks, accounts, security settings or production systems. Only authorized, capable individuals make configuration changes.

# Facility Operations & Coordination

## Maintenance requests
Church staff, ministry leaders and volunteers may report facility concerns to the Head Trustee or a designated Trustee. Trustees assess urgency, identify suitable volunteers or professionals and coordinate appropriate action.

## Projects and improvements
Projects involving building alterations, equipment installations, electrical service, permanent mounting or other facility changes should be coordinated with Trustees and affected ministry leaders. Major spending or substantive property changes require established approvals.

## Seasonal maintenance
The Trustee Committee supports winter readiness, grounds care, heating preparation, drainage and related seasonal needs. The Head Trustee coordinates these responsibilities and communicates important concerns to church leadership.

## Building emergencies
The Head Trustee coordinates the Trustee response to significant property concerns, including heating failure, leaks, equipment problems and damage. Trustees and qualified volunteers assist as able, and affected ministries and the Governing Board are informed as appropriate. **Detailed emergency actions remain in the separate Trustee Winter Emergency Plan and Prep procedure**, not in these role descriptions.

## Contractors and professional work
The Head Trustee helps arrange qualified contractors when needed, within the spending and commitment authority established by church governance. Volunteers should not perform hazardous or regulated work beyond their capabilities or legal authorization.

## Working with other ministries
Trustees work with Sunday Services and Production on space and equipment installation; Children and Youth Ministries on classroom needs; Kitchen and Hospitality on physical facilities; Security & Safety on building access and lighting; and church staff on upkeep and cleaning. Ministry leaders retain responsibility for their programs and assigned ministry equipment unless otherwise established.

# Shared expectations

- **Safety:** No volunteer is expected to undertake work they cannot perform safely. Use qualified help when appropriate.
- **Stewardship:** Consider long-term property care, cost, usefulness and safety.
- **Communication:** Report concerns, work status and completion to the Head Trustee.
- **Serving standards:** Church-wide membership and leadership expectations are maintained centrally instead of repeated in individual role descriptions.
- **Financial limits:** Job titles do not independently create budget, contracting or purchasing powers.

# Paid Custodian — Separate Employment Documentation

**Role Type:** Paid employment position (not included as a Trustee volunteer role)  
**Direct Manager:** Head Trustee

The Head Trustee directs custodial priorities, schedules and routine work oversight and provides feedback. The Custodian's specific cleaning duties, expected hours, compensation, applicable employment policies, performance process, and employment or service agreement belong in a **separate controlled employment document**. The church's authorized leadership retains decisions regarding hiring, dismissal, pay or contractual terms as determined by governing documents and employment procedures.

**No Custodian employment agreement or job description is created or amended by this working draft.**

# Review and Approval

This document describes existing and proposed responsibilities; it does not create a new church office, supersede the adopted bylaws or grant unapproved financial or employment authority.
