## Outreach Coordinator (Draft)
The Outreach Coordinator organizes and implements programs that allow the church to serve the needs in the local community.

### Core Responsibilities
- Work with the Pastoral Ministry leadership to ensure that the vision and purpose of the church remain the focus.
- Work with a team of volunteers to build conduits from the church into the community with specific goals (for example, weekly outreach to local elementary schools; food pantry, onsite or in conjunction with other local ministries; prison care ministry).
- Create opportunities for members of church to connect to serving opportunities (for example, organize meals for people who recently had surgery or are bereaved; work with deacons to visit local shut-ins or assisted-living centers; annual food drive or backpack drive for the start of school).
- Work with Pastoral Ministry leadership to identify opportunities that could be connected to the mission and vision of the church and create an ongoing outreach.

### Time Commitment
This position should require 10–20 hours per week, but this will depend on the specific needs of the church. Time commitment includes:
- Meetings to discuss, plan, and implement ongoing outreach (2–3 hours)
- Outreach setup/oversight (3–4 hours)
- Team recruiting, training, and deployment (1–2 hours)
- Annual leadership conference provided by the church (3–5 hours)
- Quarterly meetings with other Outreach Team members (1–3 hours)
- Yearly commitment to be renewed annually, if so desired
