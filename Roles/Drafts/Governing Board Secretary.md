# Governing Board Secretary

**Team:** Governance and Administration  
**Type:** Elected church officer  
**Term:** Two years under the adopted 2024 bylaws  
**Reports to:** Governing Board  
**Status:** Working draft — not adopted

## At a glance

The Governing Board Secretary helps Riverwood stay organized and accountable. This person keeps records of church decisions, tracks important deadlines, and helps the Board follow the church's bylaws and policies.

## What you will do

- Prepare, record, share, and keep minutes from Governing Board meetings.
- Keep the official records of motions, approved policies, and important Board decisions.
- Work with the Board Chair to prepare agendas and meeting information.
- Maintain the official bylaws, policy documents, and records of changes.
- Maintain the Church Business Calendar, including meetings, reports, and key deadlines.
- Coordinate the church's annual reporting to The Christian and Missionary Alliance National Office.
- Coordinate required filings and annual reports with Michigan LARA and make sure needed information is gathered from the right people.
- Help organize the Annual Meeting and other congregational business meetings, including notices, meeting materials, and official records.
- Watch for potential conflicts with the bylaws or approved policies and bring concerns to the Governing Board.
- Keep records organized so the next Secretary can take over smoothly.
- Carry out other related administrative duties assigned by the Governing Board.

## What you can decide

The Secretary may organize records, coordinate deadlines, request needed information, and correct non-substantive document errors when authorized.

The Secretary **does not independently change bylaws, adopt policies, reverse Board decisions, or discipline church members.** Matters that need Board approval must be brought to the Board.

## Working with others

The Secretary works with the Lead Pastor, Governing Board, Treasurer, other officers, committees, and ministry leaders to gather accurate information and keep governance tasks on schedule.

## What helps you succeed

Organization, careful recordkeeping, good communication, reliability, and respect for private information. Additional tools and training can be identified before this role description is adopted.
