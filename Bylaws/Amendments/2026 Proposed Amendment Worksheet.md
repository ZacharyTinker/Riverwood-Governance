## Details of Each Amendment

### Amendment 1: Small Groups and Adult Ministries

**Proposal:** Rename and expand the existing appointed Small Group Director to **Small Groups & Adult Ministries Director** under the existing annual appointment and confirmation process. Remove Alliance Men Coordinator and Alliance Women Coordinator from elected church offices. The new director will personally fill the formerly allocated Missions Committee representation; no substitute representative is authorized for this seat. Supporting coordinators may be designated as ministry needs require without becoming elected church officers.

**Affected provisions:** Article 7 (elected and appointed officers); Article 9 (Disciplemaking Ministries and Missions Committee).

**Governance Authority recommendation:** Pending.

### Amendment 2: Unified Deacon/Deaconess and Care Team

**Proposal:** Replace the separate Head Deacon and Head Deaconess elected offices with **one elected Head Deacon or Head Deaconess**, open to eligible men or women, retaining the two-year term and existing Governance Authority seat. Consolidate the deacon and deaconess teams into a unified Care Team, without separate minimum team-size requirements. Explicitly recognize Food Pantry, Ushers and Security, Welcome Team, congregational care, and other assigned care ministries. The Head Deacon or Head Deaconess administers the Benevolent Fund; coordinators manage their ministry areas and ensure scheduling is arranged. The Security Coordinator or Head Deacon/Deaconess may present security policy proposals, but adoption belongs to the Governance Authority.

**Affected provisions:** Article 6 (Governance Authority membership); Article 7 (elected offices); Article 9 (Deacons/Deaconesses and Salary Committee).

**Governance Authority recommendation:** Pending.

### Amendment 3: Church Policy Manual

**Proposal:** Add authorization in Article 6 for an official Church Policy Manual, maintained by the Governing Board Secretary and adopted or revised by Governing Board vote at a properly constituted meeting. The Manual will address operational responsibilities, board meeting cadence, parliamentary procedures, voting procedures where legally authorized, financial administration, ministry processes, and security procedures. It shall not override the bylaws, denomination requirements, or applicable law. This amendment establishes authority for the Manual; it does not automatically adopt any particular voting rule or standalone policy.

**Affected provision:** Article 6 (new Church Policy Manual section).

**Governance Authority recommendation:** Pending.

## Detailed Before and After — Every Affected Provision

The proposed text below is **consolidated** and assumes Amendments 1–3 all pass and the technical corrections are confirmed. Article 7 and the Missions Committee clause contain changes shared by more than one item; if only some amendments pass, those clauses must be reconciled before publication of a final adopted version.

### Article 6 — Governance Authority membership (A2)

**Current (January 2024):**

> - The Governance Authority shall consist of all pastors on staff, licensed by The Great Lakes District of The Christian and Missionary Alliance, elders, Head Deacon, Head Trustee, Disciplemaking Ministries Director, Secretary and Treasurer. This Board shall consist of at least nine members. 

**Proposed:**

> - The Governance Authority shall consist of all pastors on staff, licensed by The Great Lakes District of The Christian and Missionary Alliance, elders, Head Deacon or Head Deaconess, Head Trustee, Disciplemaking Ministries Director, Secretary and Treasurer. This Board shall consist of at least nine members. 

---

### Article 6 — Church Policy Manual (new provision) (A3)

**Current (January 2024):**

> (No Church Policy Manual provision exists in Article 6.)

**Proposed:**

> ### Church Policy Manual
> - The Governance Authority shall establish and maintain an official Church Policy Manual containing administrative policies, responsibilities, and operational procedures for the Governance Authority, church committees, and ministries.
> - Policies in the Church Policy Manual may be adopted, amended, or repealed by vote of the Governance Authority at a properly constituted meeting, in accordance with applicable voting requirements. The Manual may address meeting schedules, parliamentary procedures, in-person and electronic voting procedures where authorized, financial administration, ministry operations, and safety and security policies.
> - No policy or procedure shall supersede or conflict with these Bylaws, applicable law, or the governing documents of The Christian and Missionary Alliance. The Church Policy Manual shall not amend these Bylaws.
> - The Governing Board Secretary shall maintain the current Church Policy Manual and a record of its adoption, revisions, and associated Governing Board actions.

---

### Article 7 — Elected officers and terms (A1, A2, T1)

**Current (January 2024):**

> - The following officers of the church will be elected at the Annual Meeting for a two-year term: Elders, Head Deacon, Head Deaconess, Treasurer, Assistant Treasurer, Head Trustee, Governing Board Secretary. The following officers of the church will be elected at the Annual Meeting for a one-year term:  Alliance Men Coordinator, Alliance Women Coordinator, and Missions Coordinator.

**Proposed:**

> - The following officers of the church will be elected at the Annual Meeting for a two-year term: Elders, Head Deacon or Head Deaconess (one elected office), Treasurer, Assistant Treasurer, Head Trustee, Governing Board Secretary, and Missions Coordinator.

### Article 7 — Appointed ministry directors (A1)

**Current (January 2024):**

> - The following officers of the church will be appointed annually by the Lead Pastor and confirmed by the Elders and then the Governing Board: Disciplemaking Ministries Director, Worship Ministry Director, Children’s Ministry Director, Youth Ministry Director, and Small Group Director.

**Proposed:**

> - The following officers of the church will be appointed annually by the Lead Pastor and confirmed by the Elders and then the Governing Board: Disciplemaking Ministries Director, Worship Ministry Director, Children’s Ministry Director, Youth Ministry Director, and Small Groups & Adult Ministries Director.

### Article 9 — Deacons, Deaconesses, and Care Team (A2)

**Current (January 2024):**

> ### Deacons
> - The Head Deacon will be elected at the Annual Meeting for a two-year term. The Head Deacon will serve as chair of the deacons.
>
> - The Head Deacon shall create a Deacon Committee with a minimum of four deacons. The names of the deacons shall be presented to the Governing Board for confirmation. They shall serve under the direction of the Head Deacon.
>
> - The Deacons will handle the Benevolent Fund to respond to charitable needs as they arise. 
>
> ### Deaconesses 
> - The Head Deaconess will be elected at the Annual Meeting for a two-year term. The Head Deaconess will serve as chair of the deaconesses.
>
> - The Head Deaconess shall create a Deaconess Committee with a minimum of four deaconesses. The names of the deaconesses shall be presented to the Governing Board for confirmation. They shall serve under the direction of the Head Deaconess. 

**Proposed:**

> ### Deacons, Deaconesses, and Care Team
> - One Head Deacon or Head Deaconess shall be elected at the Annual Meeting for a two-year term. This single office may be filled by an eligible man or woman, serving under the title Head Deacon or Head Deaconess, respectively. The elected officer shall lead and oversee the Care Team.
> - The Care Team shall oversee ministries of practical service, congregational care, hospitality, and benevolence, including the Food Pantry, Ushers and Security, Welcome Team, and other care-related ministries as established by the Governance Authority.
> - The Food Pantry shall be coordinated by its designated Food Pantry Coordinator(s) and volunteers. Ushers and Security shall be coordinated by a Security Coordinator and associated team leads and volunteers. The Care Team may include additional coordinators, team leads, and volunteers as ministry needs require. These positions are not separate elected church offices.
> - Coordinators shall ensure that volunteer scheduling and ministry coverage are arranged for their assigned areas, either directly or in coordination with a volunteer coordinator, church office administrator, or designated team lead, as detailed in the Church Policy Manual.
> - Security-related policy and procedure proposals may be presented to the Governance Authority by the Security Coordinator or by the Head Deacon or Head Deaconess. Such policies and procedures shall require adoption by the Governance Authority.
> - The Head Deacon or Head Deaconess shall administer the Benevolent Fund in accordance with the Church Policy Manual and policies adopted by the Governance Authority.

They coordinate with each other as needed or directed, and are accountable to the Lead Pastor.

## Technical Corrections — Not Congregational Ballot Items

**Technical 1 — Missions Coordinator term (Article 7):** Article 9 already states that the Missions Coordinator is elected for a **two-year term**, and the 2024 Amendment Worksheet records approval of Amendment 16 regarding two-year officer terms. Article 7 mistakenly places this office in the one-year list. The proposed Article 7 wording places it in the two-year list and removes the conflicting one-year sentence. This is included in the working copy as a conforming edit; **confirm through the Governance Authority and notify the membership under Article 13** rather than submitting it as a new congregational amendment.

**Technical 2 — Lead Pastor title (Article 9, Missions Committee):** The 2024 Amendment Worksheet records approval of Amendment 1 changing Senior Pastor to Lead Pastor. One residual Senior Pastor reference remains in the Missions Committee clause. The proposed wording corrects that title only, without changing the committee’s other existing provisions beyond Amendment 1.

## Scope and Adoption Notes

- The 2024 adopted bylaws remain unchanged, as do the pre-existing 2026 draft, policies, and procedures.
- Only the provisions displayed in this worksheet have been altered in the separate proposed working copy; unrelated text, spelling, references, and governance requirements are intentionally unchanged.
- Each substantive amendment requires action under Article 13; the technical corrections require the Board confirmation and membership notification provided there.
- The proposed copy is **not** an adopted or operative bylaw document. Prepare a final adopted text only after the outcomes and any necessary conforming changes are known.
