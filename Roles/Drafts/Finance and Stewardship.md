# Finance & Stewardship — Ministry Roles & Responsibilities

**Riverwood Community Church**  
**Status:** Working draft — not adopted  
**Last updated:** November 20, 2026  
**Approval record:** Not yet approved

## Purpose

The Finance & Stewardship Ministry helps Riverwood responsibly manage the money and resources entrusted to the church, including donations, banking, bills, reimbursements, financial records, ministry budgets and financial reporting. It supports Riverwood's mission of connecting to people and connecting people to Jesus.

## Financial Leadership & Authority

The **Governing Board** has final authority over church finances, including approved budgets, additional funding, budget changes and financial decisions requiring Board action, subject to applicable governing and donor restrictions.

The **Treasurer** oversees financial administration, accountability, reporting and financial systems. The Treasurer advises ministry leaders and the Board about funding options but does not independently redirect another ministry's funds or approve unbudgeted spending.

Each ministry has a **budget owner** who manages spending within its approved allocation without obtaining separate approval for every individual purchase.

Authorized financial volunteers handle designated tasks such as offering counts, records and administrative assistance. No role has unlimited financial authority.

# Church Officers

## 1. Treasurer

**Role Type:** Elected church officer  
**Serving Classification:** Church leadership  
**Accountability:** Governing Board  
**Term:** Two years

### Purpose
Oversee Riverwood's financial operations so funds and transactions are recorded accurately and resources are responsibly administered.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate church financial administration, banking, deposits, payments and records.
- Oversee donation and income recording, bill payments, reimbursements and reconciliations.
- Oversee **Zeffy** and **Planning Center Giving** administration and other authorized financial systems.
- Monitor income, expenses, overall financial standing and available resources.
- Help ministry leaders understand their budgets and identify possible funding when requests exceed available amounts.
- Prepare financial reports for the Governing Board and congregation as required.
- Coordinate budget preparation with ministry leaders and the Finance Committee.
- Work with the Assistant Treasurer on donor records and contribution receipts.
- **Chair both the Finance Committee and Salary Committee** as established in the bylaws.
- Support the Finance Committee's duty to obtain an annual financial records review.
- Communicate significant concerns or discrepancies to the Governing Board.
- Perform Governing Board responsibilities of the elected office.

### Decision-Making Authority
Oversees routine financial administration and processes properly authorized payments. May recommend budget changes and funding options, but **may not unilaterally increase or redirect ministry budgets, authorize overbudget/unbudgeted expenditures, or approve another person's reimbursement against a ministry budget without the budget owner's approval**. The Governing Board decides changes requiring additional funding.

### Service Commitment
Ongoing financial work, committee leadership, reporting and annual budget preparation.

## 2. Assistant Treasurer

**Role Type:** Elected church officer  
**Serving Classification:** Church leadership  
**Accountability:** Treasurer, subject to duties established in the bylaws  
**Term:** Two years

### Purpose
Support church financial administration, particularly contribution records and annual donor receipts.

### Responsibilities
- Prepare and distribute annual donor contribution receipts **by January 31** for the preceding calendar year as directed by the adopted bylaws.
- Help maintain accurate donor records and reconcile contributions.
- Support Zeffy, Planning Center Giving and other systems as assigned.
- Investigate and help resolve contribution discrepancies.
- Protect private donor and financial information.
- Assist with authorized financial administration.
- Serve on the Finance Committee.

### Decision-Making Authority
May coordinate assigned records and donor receipts, but not change ministry budgets or independently authorize unbudgeted spending.

### Service Commitment
Work throughout the year, especially during year-end giving reconciliation and receipt preparation.

# Financial Volunteers & Committees

## 3. Financial Counter

**Role Type:** Volunteer  
**Serving Classification:** Financial volunteer  
**Accountability:** Treasurer or designated finance representative

### Purpose
Help ensure cash, checks and other physical offerings are counted and documented accurately.

### Responsibilities
- Count and verify offerings using established procedures.
- Document totals and designated giving as assigned.
- Assist with deposit preparation where authorized.
- Report discrepancies and protect donor confidentiality.
- Work with other counters as required by the church's procedures.

### Decision-Making Authority
May perform assigned counting, verification and documentation only; may not authorize expenses or independently alter records outside the assignment.

### Serving Guidelines
Accuracy, dependability, discretion and adherence to approved money-handling procedures.

### Service Commitment
Scheduled counts and related assigned work.

## 4. Finance Committee Member

**Role Type:** Committee member  
**Serving Classification:** Church leadership / committee service  
**Accountability:** Finance Committee, chaired by the Treasurer

### Purpose
Support financial oversight, budget planning and responsible stewardship.

### Responsibilities
- Review reports, budgets, financial needs and recommendations.
- Help develop and evaluate the annual budget.
- Discuss financial concerns and opportunities to improve accountability.
- **Ensure an annual review of financial records is obtained**, and consider its findings.
- Communicate recommendations to the Governing Board.
- Protect confidential information.

### Committee Composition
Under the adopted bylaws, the committee includes the **Treasurer (chair), Assistant Treasurer, Head Trustee and two additional congregation members**.

### Decision-Making Authority
Participates in committee decisions within its established authority; does not replace the Governing Board's final financial decisions.

### Service Commitment
Committee meetings, annual budget work and financial review activities.

## 5. Salary Committee Member

**Role Type:** Committee member  
**Serving Classification:** Church leadership / committee service  
**Accountability:** Salary Committee, chaired by the Treasurer

### Purpose
Recommend fair and responsible employee compensation.

### Responsibilities
- Review employee compensation and church financial capacity.
- Prepare annual salary recommendations and consider other needed changes.
- Submit recommendations through the applicable Finance Committee and Governing Board approval process.
- Maintain confidentiality and disclose potential conflicts of interest.

### Committee Composition
The adopted 2024 bylaws specify **Treasurer (chair), Governing Board Vice Chair, Head Deacon and two additional congregation members**. The proposed 2026 bylaws would change the Head Deacon reference to **Head Deacon or Head Deaconess**.

### Decision-Making Authority
Recommends compensation; does not independently authorize pay changes outside approved processes.

### Service Commitment
At least one meeting annually for budget-year salary recommendations and additional meetings as needed.

## 6. Annual Financial Review Coordinator

**Role Type:** Optional committee assignment, not an established office  
**Serving Classification:** As applicable  
**Accountability:** Finance Committee

### Purpose
Help organize the annual financial records review.

### Responsibilities
- Gather requested documents and coordinate with reviewers.
- Track outstanding requests and communicate review progress.
- Help convey findings and coordinate follow-up.

### Decision-Making Authority
Coordinates the process, not the findings. The reviewer must have appropriate independence, and the Finance Committee retains responsibility for obtaining the annual review.

### Service Commitment
Primarily during the annual review period.

# Financial Administration Responsibilities

These are **functions, not separate elected offices**. The Treasurer oversees them and may assign suitable work to authorized individuals.

## 7. Banking & Financial Accounts

**Purpose:** Maintain secure, accurate banking operations.

**Responsibilities:** Monitor account balances and activity; coordinate deposits and approved withdrawals; reconcile bank statements; maintain transaction records; identify anomalies; manage authorized banking access and approved account changes.

**Controls:** Bank access and transaction authority are limited to designated individuals and do not themselves create authority to spend a ministry's budget.

## 8. Bills, Payments & Reimbursements

**Purpose:** Ensure properly authorized expenses are documented and paid.

**Responsibilities:** Receive bills, invoices and reimbursement requests; confirm approved budget coverage or required Board authorization; obtain budget owner approval for another person's expense; coordinate payments; retain receipts; record expenses to appropriate accounts; track recurring obligations.

**Reimbursements:** Most Riverwood ministry purchases are made with personal funds and later reimbursed. **A budget owner may purchase within available approved funds without individual Board approval. If someone else incurs an expense against that budget, the budget owner must approve it before reimbursement is processed.** Finance processes approved reimbursements rather than independently deciding what ministries should purchase.

**Church payment cards:** Only authorized cardholders may use them. Card possession does not create spending authority. Practical expectations around confirming balances, limiting purchases to budgets the cardholder owns or oversees, retaining receipts and submitting documents belong in a separate **Financial Procedures Guide**.

## 9. Donations & Contribution Records

**Purpose:** Keep reliable records of charitable gifts.

**Responsibilities:** Record cash, checks and electronic giving; maintain donor records; track designated funds; reconcile donations with bank activity; correct errors through authorized processes; answer contribution questions; support annual donor receipts; protect donor information.

**Controls:** Donor-restricted money must be handled in keeping with applicable restrictions and governing requirements.

## 10. Zeffy Administration

**Purpose:** Maintain Zeffy for authorized donations, fundraising and payment activities.

**Responsibilities:** Maintain appropriate administrators; support approved donation forms, campaigns and event transactions; coordinate with ministry leaders; reconcile transactions and deposits; classify gifts; handle discrepancies and support account security.

**Authority:** Platform access or the ability to create a campaign does not independently authorize fundraising, new spending or a new budget.

## 11. Planning Center Giving Administration

**Purpose:** Maintain accurate church contribution records and giving operations.

**Responsibilities:** Administer authorized account permissions; review and record contributions; maintain donor records and giving categories; reconcile entries with deposits and bank records; find duplicate, missing or misclassified transactions; support giving statements and resolve record discrepancies.

**Authority:** Only authorized personnel may administer sensitive financial data and settings.

## 12. Budget Planning & Financial Reporting

**Purpose:** Support responsible financial planning and clear reporting.

**Responsibilities:** Collect ministry budget requests; prepare draft budgets for committee and Board consideration; track actual income and expenditures against allocations; report budget availability and commitments; identify recurring costs and expected needs; help evaluate additional funding requests against checking, savings, giving, future bills and project commitments; prepare appropriate Board and congregational reports.

**Boundary:** Financial administration does not replace each budget owner's control of routine spending within the approved allocation. The Board has final authority over budgets and changes.

## 13. Payroll & Compensation Administration

**Purpose:** Accurately process approved employee compensation and related obligations.

**Responsibilities:** Coordinate approved payroll and provider services; maintain appropriate records; record financial transactions; apply authorized compensation changes; support required reporting; safeguard confidential information.

**Boundary:** The Salary Committee recommends compensation through the bylaw process. Finance does not independently authorize hiring, pay changes or employment terms. The Head Trustee directly manages the **paid Custodian**, whose separate employment documentation controls the position's duties and terms.

# Budget Ownership & Spending Authority

## Approved Ministry Budgets

Every ministry has a designated budget owner. Once a ministry's budget is approved, that owner may make purchases and authorize expenditures within the available allocation **without separate approval for each purchase**.

The budget owner is responsible for monitoring the remaining balance and avoiding commitments beyond the approved allocation. The Treasurer maintains records and can help provide budget information.

## Purchases & Reimbursements

When a budget owner purchases within the available approved budget, no further Governing Board approval is required.

When **another person** makes a purchase or seeks reimbursement against that ministry's budget, the **budget owner must approve the charge before reimbursement is processed**.

Finance verifies and pays authorized transactions. The Treasurer cannot independently approve another person's purchase against a ministry budget over the budget owner's objection.

## Unbudgeted or Overbudget Expenditures

An expense that lacks an approved budget allocation or would exceed the amount available **requires Governing Board approval before the church commits to the purchase**, except under the emergency provision below.

The Treasurer and requesting ministry leader may identify funding options and describe the impact. The Board may:
- Add funding based on available church resources.
- Reallocate approved budget amounts from other areas.
- Split an expense across appropriate budgets.
- Postpone a purchase.
- Deny the request.

Decisions may consider checking and savings, giving trends, expected contributions, upcoming obligations and planned projects. Approval is not automatic.

## Budget Changes & Reallocations

The **Governing Board** retains final authority over changes, increases, decreases and reallocations of budgets, subject to applicable donor restrictions and governing requirements. The Treasurer may recommend changes but cannot independently move funds between ministry budgets.

Affected budget owners should be consulted when practical, especially if their allocation may be reduced. Consultation is courtesy and good leadership, **not a budget-owner veto of a Board decision**.

## Emergency Expenditures

When an immediate emergency threatens personal safety, church property or essential building operations, and delaying action would reasonably increase danger or damage, the **Head Trustee** may arrange necessary emergency services and immediate stabilization without waiting for a Board vote.

Examples include a building flood requiring a plumber, a heating failure threatening frozen pipes or an urgent electrical or structural hazard.

Emergency authority extends only to work reasonably necessary to address the immediate threat, stabilize conditions and prevent further damage. The Head Trustee must inform the **Governing Board as soon as reasonably possible**, explaining what happened, action taken, and known or anticipated costs, then update the Board as evaluations and estimates develop.

After stabilization, additional nonurgent work that needs funding beyond the approved budget returns to the normal Board approval process. The Treasurer records and processes properly authorized emergency expenses.

## Governing Board Financial Decisions

The Governing Board has final church financial authority under the governing documents. It may approve, modify, postpone or decline financial requests.

Board decisions may occur at an authorized meeting or through a properly authorized electronic voting process, including email or SMS **only if permitted** by governing requirements and applicable policy. A group conversation alone is not proof of a valid vote; the action must meet procedural requirements and be recorded.

# Financial Integrity & Accountability

## Financial controls
Maintain practical safeguards appropriate to a smaller church, separating receiving, counting, recording, approving and reconciling funds wherever possible. Procedures should reduce errors and misuse without making routine purchases unnecessarily bureaucratic.

## Access to financial systems
Limit bank, Zeffy, Planning Center Giving and other financial system permissions to people with legitimate authorized responsibilities; review access when roles change.

## Donor confidentiality
Protect individual giving records and disclose them only to authorized individuals with a necessary financial responsibility.

## Annual financial review
The Finance Committee must obtain an annual review of the church's financial records as required by the bylaws, with appropriate independence and reporting of significant findings.

## Financial reporting
Provide useful, accurate information about finances and ministry spending while protecting donor and personnel confidentiality.

## Separate Financial Procedures Guide
Day-to-day steps for counting offerings, making deposits, checking balances, submitting reimbursements and receipts, using church cards, accessing giving platforms, processing payroll and reconciling accounts should be maintained **separately** from these enduring role descriptions.
