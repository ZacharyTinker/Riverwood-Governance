# Governing Board Operations

**Riverwood Community Church**  
**Status:** Working draft — not adopted  
**Proposed approval:** Governing Board

## Purpose

This policy sets a consistent schedule and practical standards for Governing Board meetings, records, and routine administrative work.

The Governing Board (also called the Governance Authority in the bylaws) acts within the authority granted by the Church Bylaws and applicable governing requirements.

## Regular meetings

The Governing Board ordinarily meets on the **second Tuesday of each month at 7:00 PM**, except July.

**No regular Board meeting is scheduled in July.**

The Board may reschedule a meeting or hold an additional meeting when needed, following applicable meeting and notice requirements. The date and location of each meeting should be communicated to Board members.

## Parliamentary procedure

Board meetings will use the current edition of **Robert's Rules of Order Newly Revised** as their parliamentary guide, subject to the bylaws and properly adopted rules.

The Board should also confirm any needed bylaw adoption of Robert's Rules as its formal parliamentary authority. This draft policy alone does not amend the bylaws.

Meetings should allow clear motions, fair discussion, and an accurate record of decisions without unnecessary formality.

## Agendas and minutes

The Chair and Governing Board Secretary coordinate meeting agendas and materials.

The Secretary records motions, decisions, and minutes, provides minutes for Board review, and preserves approved records.

The Board may request updates from church officers, ministry directors, committees, or other responsible leaders.

## Business calendar

The Secretary maintains an annual Church Business Calendar covering meetings, elections, member notices, annual reports, appointments, financial planning, and recurring administrative deadlines.

The calendar should be checked and updated as needed. It is an administrative schedule, not a substitute for any requirement in the bylaws.

## Decisions outside a meeting

Any Board action taken outside a meeting must be authorized by applicable law and governing documents. The existing [Electronic Voting document](../Electronic%20Voting.md) remains a separate draft pending confirmation of its adoption and legal authority.

This section does **not** adopt that document or independently authorize email, text-message, or other out-of-meeting voting.

## Annual review

The Board may review its meeting schedule and operating practices as needed. Changes to this policy require the approval set out in the Policy Administration policy.
