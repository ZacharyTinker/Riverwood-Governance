# Riverwood Church Policy Manual — Master Index

**Status:** Working index — not adopted  
**Church:** Riverwood Community Church of the Christian and Missionary Alliance  
**Custodian (proposed):** Governing Board Secretary

## What this is

This page is the proposed table of contents for Riverwood's Church Policy Manual. The manual is made up of **separate documents** so each policy can be reviewed and changed without rewriting an entire book.

**Nothing listed as a draft is an approved church policy.** A policy becomes effective only after the required approval is documented. The proposed authority to establish a Policy Manual is itself part of the pending 2026 bylaw amendments.

## Proposed document control

When a document is adopted, record:
- Policy or role title
- Board approval date and motion reference
- Effective date
- Last revision date and motion reference
- Responsible team or officer
- Whether it replaces an older policy

The Governing Board Secretary would maintain this index and the official approved documents. Everyday checklists and schedules may be maintained separately without changing a role's approved authority.
