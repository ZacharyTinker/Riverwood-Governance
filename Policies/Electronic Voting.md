# Policy on Electronic Voting Outside of a Regularly Scheduled Meeting

Riverwood Community Church of the Christian and Missionary Alliance  
*Governing Board Policy | Proposed [Month Year]*

---

## Adoption Note

The bylaws of Riverwood Community Church (2024, Article 6) establish quorum and authority for the Governing Board but do not currently address voting conducted outside of a regularly scheduled in-person meeting. Robert's Rules of Order, Newly Revised (12th ed.) specifies that action by vote outside of a meeting, including by mail or email, is not valid unless expressly authorized in the bylaws or by a properly adopted special rule of order (RONR 12th ed., pp. 424, 482).

This document may be adopted in one of two ways:

1. **As a Special Rule of Order**, adopted by a two-thirds vote of the Governing Board at any properly called meeting, effective immediately upon adoption. No advance notice to the congregation is required for board-level special rules.

2. **As a Bylaw Amendment to Article 6**, following the process outlined in Article 13 of the bylaws: written submission to the Governance Authority at least 30 days prior to a congregational business meeting, a recommendation from the Governance Authority, and a two-thirds vote of members present at that meeting.

The Governing Board should determine which path is appropriate based on the intended permanence and scope of this policy. A bylaw amendment is recommended for long-term, durable authorization.

## Article [X] - Electronic Voting Outside of Meeting

*[If adopted as a bylaw amendment, insert this article after Article 6 or as a new section within Article 6. If adopted as a special rule of order, retain the format below.]*


### Section 1 - Purpose

This policy establishes the procedures by which the Governing Board of Riverwood Community Church may conduct and record official votes using electronic means, including email, when convening an in-person meeting is not feasible due to time constraints, urgency, geographic limitations, or other circumstances that make a regular meeting impractical.

Electronic voting under this policy is not a substitute for regular in-person Governing Board meetings. It is reserved for circumstances in which a timely decision is necessary and cannot reasonably wait for a scheduled meeting.

### Section 2 - Applicability

This policy applies exclusively to members of the Governing Board as defined in Article 6 of the bylaws. It does not apply to congregational votes, annual meetings, or any vote requiring congregational participation.

### Section 3 - Authority to Call an Electronic Vote

An electronic vote may be initiated by the Board Chair. The Board Chair is responsible for determining that the matter at hand meets the threshold for use of this procedure, that the motion is clearly defined prior to distribution, and that the vote is conducted in accordance with this policy.

In the event the Board Chair is unavailable or is the subject of the matter being voted on, the Lead Pastor or a designee confirmed by majority agreement of available board members may serve in this capacity for that vote.

### Section 4 - Quorum Requirement

A quorum for an electronic vote shall be the same as for an in-person Governing Board meeting: a majority (50%) of the current members of the Governing Board, as established in Article 6 of the bylaws.

An electronic vote is not valid unless a quorum of board members have submitted a response. Responses of "Abstain" count toward quorum but do not count as a vote in favor of or against the motion.

### Section 5 - Procedures for Conducting an Electronic Vote

#### 5.1 Initiating the Vote

The Board Chair shall send a written communication, by email or another documented digital means, to all members of the Governing Board. This communication must include:

- A clear and complete statement of the motion being voted on
- The relevant background or context necessary for informed voting
- The deadline by which votes must be received, which shall be no less than 48 hours from the time the communication is sent unless an emergency necessitates a shorter window, at which point the rationale for the shortened window must be stated in the message
- Instructions for how to cast a vote
- The email address or contact information of the Board Chair and the Governing Board Secretary, both of whom must receive the vote directly

#### 5.2 Casting a Vote

Each board member shall submit their vote as one of the following: **Yes**, **No**, or **Abstain**.

Votes must be submitted by replying to the communication from the Board Chair. At minimum, each member's vote must be received by both of the following:

- **The Board Chair**, who calls the vote and announces the result
- **The Governing Board Secretary**, who maintains the official record

Board members are encouraged to use the reply-all function when submitting their vote, so that the full board is aware of responses as they are received. This practice most closely replicates the transparent environment of an in-person meeting. Reply-all is encouraged but not required.

#### 5.3 Additional Commentary

Board members may include additional commentary or reasoning with their vote. Commentary is permitted but is not required. Commentary included in a reply-all response becomes part of the shared record of that vote. Commentary submitted only to the Board Chair and Secretary is retained in the record but does not circulate to the full board unless the member chooses to share it broadly.

### Section 6 - Announcing the Result

Upon the deadline, or once a quorum has voted and the outcome is no longer in question, the Board Chair shall announce the result to all Governing Board members. The announcement shall include:

- The text of the motion that was voted on
- The final vote count (Yes, No, Abstain)
- Whether quorum was achieved
- Whether the motion passed or failed

If quorum is not achieved by the stated deadline, the vote fails and the matter must be brought to the next regularly scheduled meeting.

### Section 7 - Record Keeping

The Governing Board Secretary shall retain complete documentation of each electronic vote, including:

- The original communication initiating the vote, including the full text of the motion
- All individual vote responses received, including the name of each respondent, their vote, and the date and time received
- The final announcement of the result from the Board Chair
- Any commentary submitted by board members

This documentation shall be preserved as part of the official minutes and records of the Governing Board and shall be reported at the next regularly scheduled meeting of the board.

### Section 8 - Reporting at the Next Meeting

At the next regularly scheduled meeting of the Governing Board following any electronic vote, the Board Chair or Governing Board Secretary shall formally report the vote for inclusion in the meeting minutes. The motion, vote count, and outcome shall be entered into the minutes as an official action of the Governing Board.

### Section 9 - Limitations

The following matters may not be decided by electronic vote under this policy and require action at a properly called in-person or officially convened meeting of the Governing Board:

- Amendments to the bylaws
- Election or removal of officers
- Personnel decisions involving the hiring or termination of staff
- Any matter requiring a congregational vote
- Any matter in which Robert's Rules of Order, Newly Revised (12th ed.) requires action at a formal meeting and for which no special authorization has been granted

### Section 10 - Consistency with Robert's Rules of Order

This policy is intended to operate in consistency with Robert's Rules of Order, Newly Revised (12th ed.), as the parliamentary authority of Riverwood Community Church. In any matter not addressed by this policy, the applicable provisions of RONR and the bylaws of Riverwood Community Church shall govern.
