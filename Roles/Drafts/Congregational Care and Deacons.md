# Congregational Care & Deacons — Ministry Roles & Responsibilities

**Riverwood Community Church**  
**Status:** Working draft — not adopted  
**Last updated:** November 20, 2026  
**Approval record:** Not yet approved

## Purpose

Congregational Care shows Christ's love through practical help, encouragement, hospitality and support in times of celebration, illness, loss and other significant needs.

The Head Deacon or Head Deaconess leads this work with Deacons, Deaconesses, Care Team members and volunteers who may take responsibility for specific assignments. **Assignments are not automatically church offices.**

**Governance distinction:** References below to a unified elected Head Deacon/Head Deaconess office and consolidated Care Team reflect the *proposed 2026 bylaws*, not an adopted change. Confirm against the governing bylaws before approving or appointing positions.

## Leadership and coordination

The Head Deacon or Head Deaconess coordinates care activities and works with the Lead Pastor and elders when needs call for pastoral attention.

Under the proposed ministry model, care oversight includes the Food Pantry and potentially Ushers/Security, subject to the final approved arrangement. **The Welcome Team belongs to Sunday Services, not the Care Team, in the agreed working structure.** Reconcile conflicting proposed bylaw language before adoption.

**Prayer Chain belongs under Prayer Ministry**, reporting to the Prayer Ministry Coordinator. Care Team members may cooperate when requests reveal practical assistance needs; no dual-reporting arrangement is proposed.

## 1. Head Deacon / Head Deaconess

**Type:** Elected church officer (proposed unified office) | **Classification:** Church leadership | **Accountable to:** Governing Board  
**Term:** Two years under proposed bylaws; confirm adopted requirements

**Purpose:** Lead practical congregational care and benevolence ministries.

**Responsibilities**
- Coordinate the Care Team, Deacons/Deaconesses and assigned care volunteers.
- Identify needs and help organize appropriate support for individuals and families.
- Assign responsibilities for care projects and special occasions.
- Administer the Benevolent Fund under approved financial and confidentiality procedures.
- Communicate important needs to the pastor and elders.
- Support related ministries placed under the office's oversight.
- Participate in Governing Board responsibilities applicable to the office.

**Authority:** Routine assignments and care coordination; financial assistance only within approved benevolence procedures and limits.

**Commitment:** Ongoing care coordination, leadership participation and response to needs as they arise.

## 2. Deacon / Deaconess

**Type:** Care ministry service/leadership role; exact selection process to confirm | **Classification:** Ministry leadership where applicable | **Accountable to:** Head Deacon / Head Deaconess

**Purpose:** Help meet practical needs with kindness and discretion.

**Responsibilities:** Serve in assigned care activities; notice and refer needs; assist special occasions, hospitality and benevolence-related projects; coordinate volunteers where asked; communicate with the Head Deacon or Head Deaconess; respect confidentiality.

**Authority:** Routine assigned activity coordination, not independent authority to commit church funds or make pastoral decisions.

**Commitment:** Based on assigned responsibilities.

**Open:** Confirm how Deacons/Deaconesses are selected and their precise classification under the adopted governance documents.

## 3. Care Team Member

**Type:** Volunteer | **Classification:** General volunteer unless separately appointed to a leadership role | **Accountable to:** Head Deacon / Head Deaconess

**Purpose:** Support people through practical acts of care.

**Responsibilities:** Help with cards, meals, flowers, gifts and special activities; share care needs appropriately; assist as available; protect private information.

**Commitment:** Flexible.

## Care Team assignments

These responsibilities may be assigned to Deacons, Deaconesses, Care Team members or other volunteers. They are **assignments**, not additional elected offices.

## 4. Special Occasions Coordinator

**Type:** Ministry assignment | **Accountable to:** Head Deacon / Head Deaconess

**Purpose:** Help recognize meaningful events in members' and families' lives.

**Responsibilities:** Arrange practical care-related elements for baptisms, baby dedications, graduations and Mother's Day flowers; coordinate with the pastor and relevant leaders; plan materials, volunteers and distribution.

**Authority:** Routine arrangements within approved plans and spending.

**Commitment:** As events arise.

## 5. Sentiment Cards Coordinator

**Type:** Ministry assignment | **Accountable to:** Head Deacon / Head Deaconess

**Purpose:** Send encouragement and care through cards and notes.

**Responsibilities:** Organize cards for birthdays, illness, sympathy and other occasions; maintain supplies; coordinate messages and mailing; safeguard personal details.

**Commitment:** Throughout the year as needed.

## 6. Communion Preparation Coordinator

**Type:** Ministry assignment | **Accountable to:** Head Deacon / Head Deaconess

**Purpose:** Prepare Communion elements and serving supplies.

**Responsibilities:** Keep supplies on hand; prepare elements before scheduled services; coordinate with the pastor, elders and Sunday Service Coordinator; organize practical help and cleanup.

**Boundary:** The Lead Pastor or elders oversee and administer Communion or designate appropriate people to do so. This assignment is logistical, not pastoral oversight.

**Commitment:** Scheduled Communion services.

## 7. Kitchen Supplies & Inventory Coordinator

**Type:** Ministry assignment | **Accountable to:** Head Deacon / Head Deaconess

**Purpose:** Keep church kitchen supplies and utensils organized and available.

**Responsibilities:** Check supplies and inventory; arrange routine replenishment through approved channels; flag damaged or missing equipment; coordinate needs with the Kitchen Crew.

**Boundary:** Does not replace the Kitchen Crew Team Lead's event food-service leadership.

**Commitment:** Periodic checks and as-needed support.

## 8. Meal Train Coordinator

**Type:** Ministry assignment | **Accountable to:** Head Deacon / Head Deaconess

**Purpose:** Organize meal support for families facing illness, childbirth, bereavement or other needs.

**Responsibilities:** Confirm needs and preferences with the recipient; organize volunteers and delivery timing; communicate relevant dietary instructions; avoid duplicate deliveries; protect privacy; escalate unmet needs.

**Authority:** Coordinate meal schedules and volunteers within established practices.

**Commitment:** As meal support is requested.

## 9. Flowers & Gifts Coordinator

**Type:** Ministry assignment | **Accountable to:** Head Deacon / Head Deaconess

**Purpose:** Express celebration, sympathy and encouragement through flowers and other appropriate gifts.

**Responsibilities:** Coordinate purchases/delivery of flowers, edible arrangements and gifts; confirm occasions with care leadership; track expenses; avoid duplication.

**Authority:** Routine arrangements within approved spending limits.

**Commitment:** As needed.

## 10. Ride Share Coordinator

**Type:** Proposed ministry assignment | **Accountable to:** Head Deacon / Head Deaconess

**Purpose:** Help arrange transportation assistance so people can participate in church activities.

**Responsibilities:** Receive requests; connect people with willing drivers; coordinate pickup and return details; communicate changes; refer unmet needs; follow approved transportation/safety practices.

**Authority:** Routine coordination only; does not create a transportation program without approval.

**Commitment:** As requested.

**Status:** Candidate assignment, not confirmed as a standing role.

## Related ministries and boundaries

### Food Pantry

Under the proposed model, the Care Team provides oversight while the Food Pantry Coordinator manages daily work, volunteers, inventory and distributions. Detailed Pantry roles belong in their own ministry section.

### Ushers

For now, Ushers retain Care Team/Head Deacon ministry oversight while coordinating Sunday work with the Sunday Service Coordinator. Current entrance/roaming coverage remains until the dedicated Safety Team structure is actually implemented. Their full description belongs in **Sunday Services**.

### Security & Safety

The proposed structure describes Care Team oversight, with operational work led by the Security Director/Coordinator. Final authority and reporting should be checked when the Safety Team structure is developed.

### Welcome Team

The **Welcome Team is part of Sunday Services** and reports to the Sunday Service Coordinator through its Welcome Team Lead. It is not assigned to Care Team oversight in this working roles system.

### Prayer Chain

The **Prayer Chain Coordinator belongs in Prayer Ministry** and reports to the Prayer Ministry Coordinator. The role distributes members' prayer requests and may develop further under Prayer Ministry leadership. Care Team and Prayer Ministry cooperate when prayer needs also call for meals, visits or other practical support.

## General expectations

- **Care and confidentiality:** Handle personal, family, medical or financial information respectfully. Share sensitive details only with those who need them to provide appropriate assistance.
- **Pastoral matters:** Refer serious spiritual, pastoral or sensitive situations to the pastor or elders.
- **Financial stewardship:** Follow church financial and benevolence controls. Do not promise church assistance or spend funds without authority.
- **Volunteering:** Make assignments according to availability, willingness and relevant experience without turning every task into a formal office.
- **Shared serving standards:** Membership, leadership and screening requirements are held centrally, rather than copied into each role.

## Review and approval

This is a working description of existing assignments and proposed ministry structure. It does not create offices, change adopted bylaws or establish new financial authority.
