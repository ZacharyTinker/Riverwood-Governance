# Sunday Services — Ministry Roles & Responsibilities

**Riverwood Community Church**  
**Status:** Working draft — not adopted  
**Last updated:** November 20, 2026  
**Approval record:** Not yet approved

## Purpose and coordination

Sunday Services brings together the ministries and volunteers responsible for a welcoming, organized and meaningful Sunday experience. Together, the teams support Riverwood's mission of connecting to people and connecting people to Jesus.

The **Sunday Service Coordinator** coordinates the overall service experience, including flow, timing, announcements, transitions and guest-facing spaces. This is operational coordination, not blanket supervisory authority over other ministry directors. The coordinator may give reasonable operational direction within the scope of Sunday services while respecting the Lead Pastor's oversight and each ministry leader's responsibilities.

### Working reporting structure

- Sunday Service Coordinator → Lead Pastor.
- Tech Team Lead → Sunday Service Coordinator.
- Audio Lead, Visuals Lead and Livestream Lead → Tech Team Lead.
- Production volunteers → their respective specialty leads.
- Worship Ministry Director → Lead Pastor; coordinates Sunday execution with the Sunday Service Coordinator.
- Welcome Team Lead → Sunday Service Coordinator.
- Ushers → Care Team/Head Deacon for current ministry oversight; coordinate Sunday operations with Sunday Service Coordinator. Their future placement may change with the Safety Team.

**Governance note:** The proposed 2026 bylaws still place Welcome under the Care Team. The agreed *working* operating structure places Welcome wholly under Sunday Services; reconcile proposed bylaw text before adoption. These descriptions do not modify adopted bylaws.

## 1. Sunday Service Coordinator

**Type:** Ministry coordinator | **Classification:** Ministry leadership | **Accountable to:** Lead Pastor

**Purpose:** Coordinate the Sunday service experience so people can worship, connect and participate without avoidable confusion.

**Responsibilities**
- Coordinate overall Sunday service flow, scheduling, timing and transitions.
- Work with the pastor, Worship Ministry Director, Tech Team Lead, Service Hosts, Welcome Team and other participating ministries.
- **Own the Sunday announcement process:** decide which announcements are included, their timing and priority, and give the relevant information to hosts, with appropriate pastoral oversight for sensitive or significant church-wide messages.
- Coordinate unusual or special service elements and presentations.
- Help ensure the sanctuary, lobby, signs, displays and guest-facing areas are ready.
- Address operational concerns, communicate adjustments and improve the guest experience.

**Authority:** Makes routine operational decisions for Sunday services and may provide direction to participating teams as needed. Does not independently direct doctrine, sermon content, worship repertoire or ministry-wide staffing decisions.

**Commitment:** Regular Sunday involvement and preparation/coordination during the week.

## 2. Worship Ministry Director

**Type:** Appointed ministry director | **Classification:** Ministry leadership staff | **Accountable to:** Lead Pastor

**Purpose:** Lead meaningful, Christ-centered congregational musical worship.

**Responsibilities**
- Plan and lead musical worship in coordination with the pastor and Sunday Service Coordinator.
- Choose songs and arrangements, schedule and support musicians and vocalists, and organize rehearsals.
- Recruit, train and encourage team members; coordinate microphones, instruments and technical needs with production.
- Oversee special worship opportunities, including Hymn Nights.
- Keep worship consistent with Riverwood's mission and beliefs.

**Authority:** Leads musical worship and team preparation within pastoral direction, coordinating transitions and timing with the Sunday Service Coordinator.

**Commitment:** Planning, rehearsals, team communication and scheduled services.

## 3. Worship Team Member — Musician or Vocalist

**Type:** Volunteer | **Classification:** General volunteer | **Accountable to:** Worship Ministry Director

**Purpose:** Support worship with assigned instrumental or vocal parts.

**Responsibilities**
- Prepare music and attend scheduled rehearsals and sound checks.
- Perform assigned parts and follow musical direction.
- Communicate absences and preparation needs.
- Work well with production and care for church-owned equipment.

**Serving guidance:** Musical ability appropriate to the assignment helps; a willingness to practice and learn matters.

**Commitment:** Scheduled rehearsals, services and occasional special gatherings.

## 4. Hymn Night Coordinator

**Type:** Ministry assignment | **Accountable to:** Worship Ministry Director

**Purpose:** Help organize hymn-centered worship gatherings.

**Responsibilities:** Help select hymns and plan the program, coordinate musicians, communicate technical needs, prepare materials and help the event run smoothly.

**Authority:** Routine event preparation under the Worship Ministry Director.

**Commitment:** As needed for Hymn Nights.

## 5. Service Host

**Type:** Volunteer | **Classification:** General volunteer | **Accountable to:** Sunday Service Coordinator

**Purpose:** Help guide the congregation through worship, announcements and service transitions.

**Responsibilities**
- Welcome attendees and introduce assigned portions of the service.
- Review and present announcements supplied by the Sunday Service Coordinator.
- Make transitions clear, friendly and natural.
- Coordinate changes with the pastor, coordinator and production team.

**Authority:** May adjust delivery and wording without changing the intended message; refer substantive changes to the coordinator.

**Commitment:** Scheduled Sundays, with advance review of service information.

## Production & Tech Team

The intended structure has one overall Tech Team Lead and three specialty leads. **These leadership positions are proposed and may not yet be filled.** Operators can serve while the leadership structure is developed. Role descriptions define responsibilities, not equipment checklists.

## 6. Tech Team Lead

**Type:** Team lead (proposed / recruitment) | **Classification:** Ministry leadership | **Accountable to:** Sunday Service Coordinator

**Purpose:** Coordinate reliable technical production for services and events.

**Responsibilities**
- Support Audio, Visuals and Livestream Leads and the volunteers serving with them.
- Coordinate technical preparation, coverage, training and maintenance.
- Work with the Sunday Service Coordinator and Worship Ministry Director on technical needs.
- Help resolve technical problems and identify equipment, staffing and improvement needs.

**Authority:** Direct routine production work and technical adjustments; refer significant expenditure or operational changes for appropriate approval.

**Commitment:** Ongoing team coordination, preparation and support for services/events.

## 7. Audio Lead

**Type:** Specialty team lead (proposed) | **Classification:** Ministry leadership | **Accountable to:** Tech Team Lead

**Purpose:** Guide clear, consistent sound in the room and online.

**Responsibilities:** Train and coordinate Sound & Lighting Operators; support audio mixes, microphones, instruments and monitoring; prepare and maintain equipment; address problems; coordinate with worship and livestream volunteers.

**Authority:** Routine audio setup, training and adjustments.

**Commitment:** Regular preparation, team support and maintenance.

## 8. Visuals Lead

**Type:** Specialty team lead (proposed) | **Classification:** Ministry leadership | **Accountable to:** Tech Team Lead

**Purpose:** Coordinate accurate, readable and well-timed visual presentations.

**Responsibilities:** Support and train ProPresenter operators; organize lyrics, slides and other service visuals; help ensure readability and correct timing; coordinate with the pastor, coordinator, worship director and sermon-slide creator; address equipment needs.

**Authority:** Routine visual production decisions within approved content and service plans.

**Commitment:** Service preparation, team support and occasional special events.

## 9. Livestream Lead

**Type:** Specialty team lead (proposed) | **Classification:** Ministry leadership | **Accountable to:** Tech Team Lead

**Purpose:** Guide a reliable, understandable online service experience.

**Responsibilities:** Support and train camera and livestream volunteers; coordinate video switching, shots and streaming systems; work with audio/visuals; troubleshoot and improve online production.

**Authority:** Routine camera and livestream operations under the service plan.

**Commitment:** Regular preparation, training and Sunday production.

## 10. Sound & Lighting Operator

**Type:** Volunteer | **Classification:** General volunteer | **Accountable to:** Audio Lead

**Purpose:** Help everyone hear the service clearly while keeping lighting appropriate.

**Responsibilities:** Prepare and operate audio equipment; manage assigned microphones/instrument sound; monitor audio; adjust lighting as assigned; report problems and coordinate with worship and other production volunteers.

**Serving guidance:** Experience helps but is not required; training and a willingness to learn are encouraged.

**Commitment:** Assigned services, preparation and sound checks.

## 11. ProPresenter / Lyrics Operator

**Type:** Volunteer | **Classification:** General volunteer | **Accountable to:** Visuals Lead

**Purpose:** Show lyrics and other service visuals clearly and at the appropriate time.

**Responsibilities:** Review and prepare content; display lyrics, announcements and assigned slides; follow the service plan and adjust to changes; check readability and accuracy; communicate technical problems.

**Serving guidance:** Familiarity with computers helps; training is available.

**Commitment:** Scheduled services and advance preparation.

## 12. Livestream / Video Operator

**Type:** Volunteer | **Classification:** General volunteer | **Accountable to:** Livestream Lead

**Purpose:** Help online attendees see and hear the worship service clearly.

**Responsibilities:** Operate assigned cameras, switches or streaming controls; select useful shots; follow the plan; monitor stream operation; report issues; help with setup/shutdown.

**Serving guidance:** Video experience helps but is not required.

**Commitment:** Scheduled services and selected special events.

## 13. Sermon Slides Creator

**Type:** Volunteer or ministry assignment | **Classification:** General volunteer | **Accountable to:** Sunday Service Coordinator; coordinates with speaker and Visuals Lead

**Purpose:** Prepare the speaker's visual presentation when needed.

**Responsibilities:** Discuss needs with the speaker; prepare or format slides; verify readability and organization; revise as requested; provide finished material to production before the service.

**Authority:** Routine design and formatting choices; preserve the speaker's intended message.

**Commitment:** As needed.

## Welcome & Hospitality

## 14. Welcome Team Lead

**Type:** Team lead | **Classification:** Ministry leadership | **Accountable to:** Sunday Service Coordinator

**Purpose:** Organize a welcoming first experience for visitors and regular attendees.

**Responsibilities**
- Schedule, train and assist Welcome Team volunteers.
- Prepare the welcome area and help guests find locations or services.
- **Maintain new visitor bags**: monitor supplies, assemble bags and coordinate replenishment.
- Help volunteers assist parents using children's check-in.
- Work with the Sunday Service Coordinator on signs, guest needs and welcome improvements.

**Authority:** Routine welcome assignments and supply coordination within approved arrangements.

**Commitment:** Ongoing scheduling, preparation and Sunday involvement.

## 15. Welcome Team Member

**Type:** Volunteer | **Classification:** General volunteer | **Accountable to:** Welcome Team Lead

**Purpose:** Help people feel welcomed and find their way at Riverwood.

**Responsibilities**
- Greet visitors and regular attendees; answer basic questions and offer directions.
- Help guests find the sanctuary, classrooms, restrooms and other areas.
- Assist families at **Planning Center Check-Ins** kiosks, including locating/creating a household, selecting each child and appropriate room, completing check-in and collecting printed labels; refer questions or difficulties to children's ministry personnel.
- Help give visitors welcome bags when appropriate.
- Communicate guest needs to the Welcome Team Lead.

**Boundary:** Assisting with check-in is not taking custody of or supervising minors. Child supervision and release rules remain with the appropriate children's ministry.

**Commitment:** Scheduled Sunday welcoming, including before Sunday School or worship as assigned.

## 16. Usher

**Type:** Volunteer | **Classification:** General volunteer | **Accountable to:** Care Team / Head Deacon for present oversight; coordinates Sunday operations with Sunday Service Coordinator

**Purpose:** Support an orderly, safe and welcoming worship environment.

**Current responsibilities**
- Greet and assist attendees; **help guests find a seat when needed**, even though available space means this is uncommon.
- Help people locate rooms and facilities.
- Support the current safety coverage assignments and communicate concerns to appropriate leaders.
- Help with offering, materials and service activities as assigned.
- One scheduled usher records attendance using the established process.

**Current safety assignment:** Typically two ushers remain outside the service: one near the sanctuary entrance and one moving throughout the building. They may exchange positions. This continues until safety responsibilities formally move to a dedicated Safety Team.

**Potential future focus (not yet implemented):** Guest navigation, seating, service transitions and stage-management assistance such as moving equipment.

**Commitment:** Assigned services, including pre-service coverage.

## 17. Sunday School Hospitality Volunteer

**Type:** Informal volunteer signup | **Classification:** General volunteer | **Accountable to:** Sunday Service Coordinator for Sunday hospitality coordination

**Purpose:** Provide simple refreshments for Sunday School fellowship.

**Assignments (volunteers may do one or both):**
- **Donuts:** Sign up, bring donuts and have them available **before 9:30 a.m. Sunday School**.
- **Coffee:** Prepare and brew coffee, have it ready **before 9:30 a.m.**, and keep the serving area tidy.

Communicate if unable to fulfill a signup. Anyone willing to help may participate; no formal ministry appointment is needed.

## Shared expectations and status

Church-wide membership, leadership, screening and child-protection requirements are maintained centrally, not duplicated here. Baptism and Communion remain under the oversight of the Lead Pastor or elders. Individual role descriptions do not establish elected offices or supersede bylaws.

**Approval:** Working draft for ministry leadership review and future consideration; not adopted.
