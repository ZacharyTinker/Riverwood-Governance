# Missions — Ministry Roles & Responsibilities

**Riverwood Community Church**  
**Status:** Working Draft — Not Adopted

## Purpose

The Missions Ministry helps Riverwood participate in God's work of sharing the gospel and making disciples throughout the world.

The ministry connects the congregation with the worldwide work of The Christian and Missionary Alliance (C&MA) through prayer, giving, missionary relationships, missions education, and opportunities to serve.

The goal is to help missions become an ongoing part of Riverwood's church life rather than something discussed only during special events.

## Ministry Leadership & Coordination

The Missions Coordinator leads the Missions Ministry and serves as chair of the Missions Committee.

The Missions Committee supports missions activities, missionary relationships, congregational involvement, and special initiatives. Committee members and other volunteers may carry out specific responsibilities according to their abilities, interests, and availability.

### Reporting & Accountability

- The Missions Coordinator is an elected church officer serving a **two-year term**.
- The Missions Coordinator chairs the Missions Committee and works closely with the Lead Pastor.
- Missions Committee Members serve under the leadership of the Missions Coordinator.
- Volunteers assisting with specific activities report to the Missions Coordinator or a designated Committee Member.
- The Missions Coordinator coordinates with The Christian and Missionary Alliance and Great Lakes District staff when appropriate.

# Ministry Roles

## 1. Missions Coordinator

**Role Type:** Elected Church Officer  
**Serving Classification:** Church Leadership / Ministry Leadership Staff  
**Accountability:** Church governance, coordinating with the Lead Pastor  
**Term:** Two years

### Purpose

Lead Riverwood's Missions Ministry by helping the congregation learn about, pray for, financially support, and actively participate in worldwide missions.

### Responsibilities

**Missions Leadership**
- Provide overall leadership and direction for the Missions Ministry.
- Serve as chair of the Missions Committee.
- Organize Committee meetings, activities, and assignments.
- Develop opportunities for ongoing congregational involvement in missions.
- Help connect Riverwood's missions activities with the broader work of The Christian and Missionary Alliance.

**Alliance Missions Engagement**
- Oversee Riverwood's participation in Alliance Missions Engagement initiatives.
- Help the congregation understand the work of Alliance Missions, including aXcess, CAMA, Envision, and marketplace ministries.
- Share missions stories, updates, resources, and opportunities throughout the year.
- Coordinate activities and communications associated with the Alliance's current Missions Engagement theme.
- Encourage members and attendees to participate through prayer, giving, learning, and service.

**Annual Missions Conference**
- Lead the planning and coordination of Riverwood's annual Missions Conference.
- Work with Great Lakes District Office staff to coordinate conference arrangements, participating missionaries, and available resources.
- Coordinate with the Lead Pastor and Sunday Service Coordinator regarding conference worship services and presentations.
- Organize Committee members and volunteers to help with hospitality, scheduling, activities, and preparation.
- Oversee the conference's approved budget and resource needs.

**Short-Term Missions**
- Oversee the development and coordination of short-term mission trips originating from Riverwood.
- Identify appropriate opportunities through Alliance ministries and other approved missions partners.
- Coordinate with host organizations, ministry leaders, and prospective participants.
- Help organize trip planning, preparation, fundraising, and logistics.
- Ensure proposed trips receive appropriate church leadership approval.
- Help establish clear trip leadership, participation expectations, and preparation requirements.
- Coordinate with appropriate church leaders concerning participant safety, safeguarding, insurance, and emergency planning.
- Encourage post-trip reflection and opportunities for participants to share their experiences.

**Great Commission Sunday & Missions Giving**
- Coordinate Riverwood's participation in Great Commission Sunday and related Alliance giving initiatives.
- Help communicate the purpose and impact of the Great Commission Fund.
- Encourage congregational awareness of missions giving opportunities.
- Work with the Lead Pastor and Sunday Service Coordinator to plan appropriate service activities and announcements.
- Coordinate with the Treasurer concerning designated giving, financial reporting, and approved missions expenditures.

**Missionary Relationships**
- Maintain appropriate communication with missionaries and missions partners connected to Riverwood.
- Share missionary news, prayer requests, and updates when authorized.
- Help organize opportunities for the congregation to encourage missionaries.
- Coordinate missionary visits and presentations with church leadership.
- Respect confidentiality and security needs when communicating missionary information.

**Missions Communications & Visibility**
- Maintain Riverwood's Missions Wall with current, relevant, and approved information.
- Coordinate Missions Moments and other missions-related announcements.
- Provide missions information to the appropriate communications and production volunteers.
- Help maintain useful resources about Alliance Missions and opportunities for involvement.
- Identify ways to make missions visible throughout the year.

**Financial & Administrative Responsibilities**
- Manage the Missions Ministry's approved budget.
- Coordinate with the Treasurer and Finance Committee concerning missions funding and designated contributions.
- Prepare additional funding requests when necessary.
- Maintain appropriate records of major ministry activities, plans, and commitments.
- Communicate significant ministry needs and recommendations to church leadership.

### Decision-Making Authority

The Missions Coordinator may organize missions activities, coordinate Committee responsibilities, manage the approved Missions budget, and make routine ministry decisions.

The Missions Coordinator may develop and recommend mission trips, fundraising activities, partnerships, and other initiatives.

Activities requiring additional financial commitments, new funding, or broader church authorization must receive appropriate approval before proceeding.

The Missions Coordinator may not independently commit Riverwood to financial obligations or partnerships beyond the authority granted by church leadership.

### Service Commitment

Ongoing responsibilities throughout the year, including Committee coordination, missionary relationships, Alliance Missions Engagement, and preparation for special events and short-term mission opportunities.

## 2. Missions Committee Member

**Role Type:** Committee Member  
**Serving Classification:** Ministry Leadership / Committee Service  
**Accountability:** Missions Coordinator

### Purpose

Support the Missions Ministry by helping Riverwood participate in worldwide missions through prayer, giving, education, and service.

### Responsibilities

- Participate in Missions Committee meetings and planning.
- Help organize the annual Missions Conference.
- Support Alliance Missions Engagement activities.
- Assist with Great Commission Sunday and missions-related giving initiatives.
- Help coordinate missionary communication and encouragement.
- Participate in planning approved short-term mission opportunities.
- Assist with maintaining and updating the Missions Wall.
- Help prepare missions displays, presentations, and informational materials.
- Encourage opportunities for the congregation to pray, give, and participate.
- Carry out assigned Committee projects and responsibilities.
- Work cooperatively with ministry leaders and volunteers.

### Decision-Making Authority

Committee Members may coordinate assigned activities under the direction of the Missions Coordinator. They may not independently authorize unbudgeted expenditures, create financial commitments, or establish church partnerships.

### Service Commitment

Ongoing participation as needed, with additional involvement during major missions events and initiatives.

### Committee Composition

The Committee's membership is governed by Riverwood's adopted bylaws.

The proposed 2026 bylaw changes to Committee composition remain pending until formally adopted.

# Missions Ministry Responsibilities

The following are areas of work or assignments within the Missions Ministry, not separate elected positions.

## 3. Annual Missions Conference

### Purpose

Organize an annual gathering that helps the congregation connect with missionaries and the worldwide work of The Christian and Missionary Alliance.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate with Great Lakes District staff regarding conference scheduling and missionary participation.
- Work with the Lead Pastor and Sunday Service Coordinator on worship service involvement.
- Plan appropriate presentations, activities, and learning opportunities.
- Organize volunteer assignments, hospitality, and event preparation.
- Coordinate missionary accommodations, transportation, or other arrangements when needed.
- Prepare promotional and informational materials.
- Manage event expenses within the approved Missions budget.

The Missions Coordinator retains overall responsibility for the conference and may delegate individual activities to Committee Members or volunteers.

## 4. Short-Term Missions & Mission Trips

### Purpose

Create opportunities for Riverwood participants to serve, learn, and participate in missions through approved short-term experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Identify suitable short-term mission opportunities.
- Coordinate with approved missions organizations and host ministry partners.
- Help organize participant interest, trip planning, and preparation.
- Recommend appropriate trip leaders and volunteer responsibilities.
- Coordinate schedules, travel arrangements, fundraising, and communication.
- Work with church leadership to establish trip budgets and participation requirements.
- Ensure appropriate travel, safeguarding, insurance, and emergency planning arrangements are addressed.
- Help participants prepare to serve respectfully and effectively.
- Organize follow-up and opportunities to share experiences with the congregation.

### Ministry Boundary

The Missions Coordinator oversees the mission-trip program but may designate a Trip Leader for an individual trip. The Trip Leader manages assigned on-trip responsibilities within approved plans. Trip-specific responsibilities and procedures should be documented separately.

## 5. Great Commission Sunday

### Purpose

Help Riverwood participate in Great Commission Sunday and communicate the importance of supporting the worldwide mission of The Alliance.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate Great Commission Sunday activities with the Lead Pastor.
- Work with the Sunday Service Coordinator concerning the service plan and timing.
- Prepare appropriate missions information, updates, and giving opportunities.
- Use available Alliance materials when helpful.
- Encourage congregational understanding of the Great Commission Fund.
- Coordinate with Finance concerning the recording and handling of designated giving.

The Missions Coordinator is responsible for overall coordination and may delegate presentation or preparation tasks.

## 6. Missions Engagement

### Purpose

Help the congregation participate in the Alliance's broader missions emphasis throughout the year.

### Responsibilities
- Review current Alliance Missions Engagement themes and resources.
- Identify useful videos, messages, educational materials, and activity ideas.
- Coordinate missions-focused activities for adults, youth, and children when appropriate.
- Encourage prayer and financial participation.
- Share meaningful stories of Alliance missions work.
- Help members and attendees discover opportunities for involvement.
- Coordinate with other ministry leaders to incorporate missions into church activities.

Missions Engagement is an ongoing responsibility of the Missions Ministry rather than a separate ministry office.

## 7. Missions Wall & Information Displays

### Purpose

Keep missions visible and provide the congregation with current information about worldwide missions.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain Riverwood's Missions Wall.
- Update missionary information, photographs, maps, prayer requests, and related materials when appropriate.
- Remove outdated or incorrect information.
- Display approved Alliance missions resources and updates.
- Coordinate printed materials, photographs, and visual elements as needed.
- Ensure sensitive missionary information is not displayed publicly without permission.
- Help keep the display organized, attractive, and useful.

### Assignment

The Missions Coordinator is accountable for maintaining the Missions Wall but may delegate updates and upkeep to a Committee Member or volunteer.

## 8. Missions Moment Presenter

**Role Type:** Occasional Ministry Assignment  
**Accountability:** Missions Coordinator

### Purpose

Share short missions-related updates during Sunday worship services.

### Responsibilities
- Present approved missionary updates, prayer requests, stories, or opportunities.
- Coordinate the information with the Missions Coordinator.
- Work with the Sunday Service Coordinator concerning timing and scheduling.
- Provide slides or other materials to the Tech Team when needed.
- Encourage prayer, learning, or participation when appropriate.

The Missions Coordinator may present Missions Moments personally or designate a Missions Committee Member or another appropriate person. This is an assignment, not a separate standing ministry position.

## 9. Missionary Communication & Encouragement

### Purpose

Maintain meaningful connections with missionaries and missions partners.

### Responsibilities
- Share approved missionary updates with the congregation.
- Coordinate prayer requests and opportunities for encouragement.
- Help organize appropriate correspondence, cards, or other support.
- Coordinate missionary visits or virtual presentations when approved.
- Communicate significant missionary needs to the Missions Coordinator.
- Protect confidential or sensitive missionary information.

# Ministry Cooperation

## Lead Pastor

The Missions Coordinator works with the Lead Pastor to align missions activities with Riverwood's ministry direction, including missionary visits, missions giving emphases, trips, and special services.

## Sunday Services

The Missions Coordinator coordinates with the Sunday Service Coordinator when missions activities affect service announcements, presentations, or schedules. The Missions Coordinator owns missions content, while the Sunday Service Coordinator coordinates service flow.

## Finance & Stewardship

The Missions Coordinator manages the approved ministry budget and works with the Treasurer regarding missions giving, fundraising, financial records, and additional funding requests.

Purchases within approved funds do not require individual Board approval. Unbudgeted or overbudget expenses follow the church's Governing Board approval requirements.

## Community Outreach & Disciplemaking

Missions primarily supports Riverwood's participation in worldwide missions, while Community Outreach focuses on local community engagement. The ministries may collaborate when appropriate, especially on mission trips, service opportunities, and discipleship activities.

## Children, Youth & Adult Ministries

The Missions Ministry may provide age-appropriate missions materials and opportunities for involvement. Individual ministry leaders retain responsibility for their programs and participants.

## Creative & Communications

The Missions Coordinator works with communications volunteers on missions promotions, the Missions Wall, presentations, and other materials. Missionary privacy and information security must be considered before publication.

# General Ministry Expectations

## Alliance Missions Alignment

Riverwood's Missions Ministry supports and encourages involvement in the worldwide work of The Christian and Missionary Alliance, including aXcess, CAMA, Envision, and marketplace ministries.

## Missionary Privacy & Security

Missionary information must be shared responsibly. Sensitive locations, identities, ministry activities, or personal details should not be publicly displayed or communicated without appropriate permission.

## Short-Term Mission Trip Safety

Trip participation, supervision, screening, insurance, safeguarding, and emergency preparation must follow applicable church requirements and arrangements established for each trip.

The Missions Coordinator is responsible for ensuring these matters are addressed but may assign qualified individuals to coordinate specific preparations.

## Financial Stewardship

The Missions Coordinator manages the approved ministry budget and works with Finance on designated giving and financial obligations. The Governing Board retains final authority over financial decisions, additional funding, and budget changes.

## Volunteer Participation

Committee Members and volunteers may assist with specific activities according to their availability and abilities. Applicable membership, leadership, and screening requirements are maintained through Riverwood's church-wide serving standards.
