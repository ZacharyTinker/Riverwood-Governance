# Riverwood Role Directory

**Status:** Working inventory — not an adopted organization chart or set of role descriptions  
**Goal:** Make it easy for someone to understand where they could serve and what each role does.

## How to use this directory

- Roles are grouped by **team or function**, not a final chain of command.
- **Bylaws** means the role is named in the adopted 2024 bylaws. **Proposed** means it appears in a pending 2026 bylaw change. **User-listed** means it was named for this inventory. **Needs confirmation** means details or even a separate standing position are not yet settled.
- **Director:** generally an appointed ministry leader. **Coordinator:** generally works within a director-led ministry, with exceptions where approved. **Volunteer:** serves within an approved role or team.
- A team's name is not always a separate job title. A role may serve more than one team.
- A working description does not create a new office, grant Board membership, or change reporting relationships.

### Disciplemaking and Community Outreach — unresolved

The **Disciplemaking Ministries Director** is named in the bylaws. **Community Outreach Director** is the working outreach role. They overlap in practice, but should **remain separate in this directory until the final title, scope, appointment, and Board representation are decided**.

Do not rename or merge these offices in approved documents without the proper authority.
