# Policy on Electronic Voting Outside of a Regularly Scheduled Meeting

Riverwood Community Church of the Christian and Missionary Alliance  
*Governing Board Policy | *Adopted by the Governing Board on [Date]*  

---

## Adoption Note

The bylaws of Riverwood Community Church establish quorum and authority for the Governing Board but do not currently address voting conducted outside of a regularly scheduled in-person meeting. Robert’s Rules of Order, Newly Revised specifies that action by vote outside of a meeting, including by mail, email, or other electronic means, is not valid unless expressly authorized in the bylaws or by a properly adopted special rule of order.

This policy is intended to provide a clear, limited, and accountable process for electronic voting by the Governing Board when a decision is time-sensitive and cannot reasonably wait until the next scheduled meeting.

This document may be adopted in one of two ways:

1. **As a Special Rule of Order**, adopted by a two-thirds vote of the Governing Board at any properly called meeting, effective immediately upon adoption.

2. **As a Bylaw Amendment**, following the amendment process outlined in the bylaws.

The Governing Board should determine which path is appropriate based on the intended permanence and scope of this policy. A bylaw amendment is recommended for long-term, durable authorization.

## Section 1 - Purpose

This policy establishes the procedures by which the Governing Board of Riverwood Community Church may conduct and record official votes using approved electronic means when a timely decision is necessary and the matter cannot reasonably wait until the next scheduled meeting.

Electronic voting is not a substitute for regular in-person Governing Board meetings, meaningful discussion, prayerful discernment, or normal board deliberation.

Ordinarily, electronic voting shall be limited to matters that have already been discussed by the Governing Board in some form, where the remaining issue is final approval of details, wording, timing, cost, implementation, or another clearly defined action.

Electronic voting should not be used to introduce major new business, decide controversial matters, avoid discussion, or act on issues that require extended deliberation.

---

## Section 2 - Definitions

For purposes of this policy, **electronic voting** means voting conducted outside of a regular or properly called meeting through a documented digital method approved for use by the Governing Board.

Approved electronic voting methods may include email, secure group messaging platforms, board management systems, church management systems, online voting tools, forms, polling systems, or other digital platforms that allow the vote to be documented and retained as part of the official record.

References in this policy to an electronic vote, electronic vote notice, electronic vote record, written communication, written response, documented digital means, or official vote record are intended to include email and any other approved digital method capable of preserving a clear official record.

Any electronic voting method used under this policy must allow the Governing Board Secretary to verify and preserve:

- The exact motion submitted for vote
- The date and time the vote was initiated
- The full list of board members who received notice
- Each board member’s response of Yes, No, Abstain, or Non-Response
- The date and time each response was received, when available
- Any objection to electronic consideration
- Any comments, clarifications, or material revisions
- The final result announced by the Chair or Vice-Chair

A platform or method shall not be used for electronic voting if it does not allow the Governing Board Secretary to create or retain a clear and complete official record.

The Governing Board may approve specific electronic voting methods or platforms for use under this policy. Any approved method must provide sufficient documentation for the Governing Board Secretary to verify notice, quorum, vote count, objections, and final outcome.

---

## Section 3 - Applicability

This policy applies exclusively to members of the Governing Board as defined in the bylaws.

It does not apply to congregational votes, annual meetings, membership votes, or any vote requiring congregational participation.

No committee, ministry team, informal group, or subgroup of Governing Board members may conduct an electronic vote on behalf of the Governing Board unless the vote is initiated under this policy and sent to all current members of the Governing Board.

A quorum is established by participation in a properly initiated vote of the full Governing Board, not by a separate message thread, subgroup discussion, committee discussion, elder-board discussion, or informal agreement among a portion of the Governing Board.

---

## Section 4 - Prior Discussion Requirement

Electronic voting shall not be used to introduce a new matter to the Governing Board without prior discussion, except in a true emergency.

Ordinarily, a matter submitted for electronic vote must have been previously discussed by the Governing Board at a regular or properly called meeting, or otherwise circulated to the full board for review and discussion before the vote is initiated.

Electronic voting is intended for situations in which the general matter has already been considered by the board, but final details, wording, timing, costs, or implementation steps need to be confirmed before formal approval.

If the matter has not previously been discussed by the Governing Board, the initiating message must clearly state why electronic voting is necessary and why the matter cannot reasonably wait until the next regular or properly called meeting.

---

## Section 5 - Officers for Electronic Voting

For the purposes of this policy, the **Chair** shall be the Lead Pastor.

The **Vice-Chair** shall be an elder chosen by the Governing Board at the first Governing Board meeting following the elections at the Annual Meeting.

The **Governing Board Secretary** shall serve as the official process verifier and records keeper for electronic votes conducted under this policy.

The Chair or Vice-Chair initiates and oversees the electronic vote. The Governing Board Secretary verifies that the procedural requirements of this policy have been met and maintains the official record.

---

## Section 6 - Authority to Call an Electronic Vote

An electronic vote may be initiated by the Chair or Vice-Chair.

The Chair or Vice-Chair is responsible for determining that the matter meets the threshold for use of this procedure, that the motion is clearly defined prior to distribution, and that the vote is conducted in accordance with this policy.

The Governing Board Secretary is responsible for verifying that all procedural and recordkeeping requirements of this policy have been met, including full-board notice, response tracking, quorum, voting threshold, documentation, and inclusion of the result in the official records.

Because electronic voting occurs outside of a regular meeting, the Chair or Vice-Chair and Governing Board Secretary shall work together to ensure that the vote is both properly conducted and properly documented.

If the Chair is unavailable, has a conflict of interest, or is the subject of the matter being voted on, the Vice-Chair may initiate and oversee the electronic vote.

If both the Chair and Vice-Chair are unavailable, have a conflict of interest, or are the subject of the matter being voted on, the Governing Board may determine the appropriate process at a regular or properly called meeting.

---

## Section 7 - Full Board Notice Requirement

An electronic vote is valid only if the original vote notice is sent to every current member of the Governing Board through an approved electronic voting method.

The Governing Board Secretary must be included in the original vote notice and must have access to all vote responses and records.

If the Governing Board Secretary is not included in the original vote notice or does not have access to the full electronic vote record, the vote shall not be finalized until the Secretary has reviewed the full record and confirmed that the requirements of this policy were met.

If any current Governing Board member is omitted from the original vote notice, whether intentionally or unintentionally, the vote is void and must be restarted with all current Governing Board members included.

Delivery of the electronic vote to all board members does not, by itself, establish quorum. Quorum is established only when the required number of board members have submitted a response of Yes, No, or Abstain.

Before the result of an electronic vote is announced as official, the Governing Board Secretary shall review the recipient list or platform participant list, responses, voting threshold, and documentation to confirm that the requirements of this policy were met.

The Chair or Vice-Chair may announce the result only after the Governing Board Secretary has confirmed that the vote was properly conducted and properly documented.

---

## Section 8 - Quorum, Approval, and Vote Outcome Requirements

For purposes of this policy, “current voting members of the Governing Board” means those individuals officially serving as members of the Governance Authority under Article 6 of the bylaws at the time the electronic vote is initiated.

Vacant positions and non-voting participants shall not count toward quorum or voting thresholds.

Quorum for electronic voting shall be calculated according to Article 6 of the bylaws. When the calculation results in a fraction, the required number shall be rounded up to the next whole number.

Responses of Yes, No, or Abstain count toward quorum.

Non-responses do not count toward quorum and shall not be counted as Yes, No, or Abstain.

Silence shall not be interpreted as consent, opposition, or abstention. A board member who does not respond by the stated deadline shall be recorded as not responding and shall not count toward quorum or toward the vote result.

Because electronic voting occurs outside of a regular meeting, a motion shall pass only if it receives Yes votes from a majority of the full current voting membership of the Governing Board, unless the bylaws, this policy, or Robert’s Rules of Order require a higher threshold.

Even if quorum is achieved, the motion fails unless the required number of Yes votes is received by the stated deadline.

For purposes of this policy, a motion **passes** when the electronic vote was properly conducted, quorum was achieved, and the required number of Yes votes was received by the stated deadline.

For purposes of this policy, a motion **fails** when the electronic vote was properly conducted but the required number of Yes votes was not received by the stated deadline.

A vote is **void** when the electronic voting process did not meet the requirements of this policy. A void electronic vote shall be treated as though no official vote occurred.

An electronic vote may be void for reasons including, but not limited to:

- The vote was not initiated by the Chair or Vice-Chair as required by this policy
- The original vote notice was not sent to every current voting member of the Governing Board
- The Governing Board Secretary was not included in the original vote notice or did not have access to the full electronic vote record
- An unapproved electronic voting method was used
- The motion being voted on was not clearly stated
- The voting deadline was not clearly stated
- The Governing Board Secretary could not verify the recipient list, participant list, vote responses, voting threshold, or official vote record
- The matter being voted on was not eligible for electronic voting under this policy

A vote is **suspended** when a current voting member of the Governing Board objects to electronic consideration before the voting deadline, as provided in this policy. A suspended electronic vote shall not be counted as passed, failed, or void. The matter shall instead be moved to a regular or properly called meeting of the Governing Board.

Failure of a motion by electronic vote does not prevent the same motion, or a substantially similar motion, from being brought before the Governing Board at a regular or properly called meeting.

Because electronic voting occurs outside of a meeting and does not provide the same opportunity for discussion and deliberation, a failed electronic vote shall not prohibit further consideration of the matter at a regular or properly called meeting.

A motion that fails electronically may be reconsidered, revised, or brought again at a regular or properly called meeting, including with the same wording, unless prohibited by the bylaws, Robert’s Rules of Order, or a prior action of the Governing Board.

For example, if the Governing Board has 10 current voting members, 5 responses are required for quorum under the bylaws. However, the motion passes only if at least 6 members vote Yes, because 6 is a majority of the full current voting membership.

For example, if the Governing Board has 9 current voting members, 5 responses are required for quorum because 50% results in a fraction and is rounded up. The motion also requires at least 5 Yes votes to pass.

## Section 9 - Procedures for Conducting an Electronic Vote

### 9.1 Initiating the Vote

The Chair or Vice-Chair shall send a written communication, through an approved electronic voting method, to all members of the Governing Board.

The initiating communication must include:

- A clear and complete statement of the motion being voted on
- The relevant background or context necessary for informed voting
- A summary of any prior board discussion related to the matter
- The reason the matter is being handled electronically rather than waiting for the next scheduled meeting
- The deadline by which votes must be received
- Instructions for how to cast a vote
- A statement explaining that any board member may object to handling the matter electronically
- The contact information or voting method by which the Chair or Vice-Chair and Governing Board Secretary will receive and verify votes

The electronic vote must include the exact motion being voted on. The motion shall be stated clearly enough that each board member can vote Yes, No, or Abstain without needing to infer the meaning, scope, cost, authority, or implementation of the action.

### 9.2 Voting Timeline

The standard voting period for an electronic vote shall be 48 hours from the time the motion is distributed.

When the matter is not urgent, when significant details are being finalized, or when the Chair or Vice-Chair determines that additional time would be helpful, a 72-hour voting period should be used.

No electronic vote may close early unless all current Governing Board members have responded.

### 9.3 Casting a Vote

Each board member shall submit their vote as one of the following:

- Yes
- No
- Abstain

Votes must be submitted through the approved electronic voting method identified in the initiating communication.

At minimum, each member’s vote must be accessible to both of the following:

- The Chair or Vice-Chair, who initiates and oversees the vote
- The Governing Board Secretary, who verifies the process and maintains the official record

When the voting method allows responses to be visible to the full board, board members are encouraged to submit responses in a way that preserves appropriate transparency. If the voting method does not allow responses to be visible to the full board, the Governing Board Secretary shall maintain the official vote record and the Chair or Vice-Chair shall announce the final result to the full board.

### 9.4 Acknowledgment and Follow-Up

Each board member is encouraged to acknowledge receipt of the electronic vote, even if they are not yet ready to cast a vote.

If one or more board members have not responded during the voting period, the Chair, Vice-Chair, or Governing Board Secretary should make reasonable follow-up attempts before the deadline, such as a direct message, direct email, text message, phone call, or other appropriate means of contact.

A lack of response after reasonable follow-up shall be recorded as non-response and shall not count toward quorum or toward the vote result.

---

## Section 10 - Right to Object to Electronic Consideration

Any Governing Board member may object to the use of electronic voting for the matter under consideration by responding within the official electronic vote record before the voting deadline, or by notifying the Chair or Vice-Chair and Governing Board Secretary in writing before the voting deadline.

An objection should state that the member believes the matter requires additional discussion, prayerful discernment, clarification, or consideration at a regular or properly called meeting.

If any board member objects to handling the matter electronically, the electronic vote shall be suspended immediately.

Votes already submitted before the objection shall not be counted as official board action.

The matter shall then be placed on the agenda for the next regular or properly called meeting of the Governing Board.

An objection to electronic consideration is not considered a vote for or against the motion. It is an objection to the method of voting.

The Governing Board Secretary shall record the objection and retain it as part of the official record.

---

## Section 11 - Discussion, Clarification, and Material Changes

Board members may ask questions, offer comments, request clarification, or provide relevant information during the voting period using the official electronic vote record or another documented method accessible to the Chair or Vice-Chair and Governing Board Secretary.

If, during the voting period, a board member raises new information or a material concern that could reasonably affect the board’s decision, the Chair or Vice-Chair may pause or cancel the electronic vote and move the matter to a regular or properly called meeting.

If the motion is materially revised during the voting period, the voting period shall restart with the revised motion clearly stated and sent to all current Governing Board members.

Minor clarifications that do not change the substance of the motion do not require the vote to restart, provided the Chair or Vice-Chair clearly communicates the clarification to the full Governing Board.

The Governing Board Secretary shall retain any material clarifications, revisions, or cancellation notices as part of the official record.

---

## Section 12 - Additional Commentary

Board members may include additional commentary or reasoning with their vote.

Commentary is permitted but is not required.

Commentary included in the official electronic vote record becomes part of the shared record of that vote.

Commentary submitted only to the Chair or Vice-Chair and Secretary is retained in the official record but does not circulate to the full board unless the member chooses to share it broadly.

If the electronic voting method does not allow commentary within the vote itself, board members may submit commentary separately in writing to the Chair or Vice-Chair and Governing Board Secretary.

---

## Section 13 - Secretary Verification and Announcement of Result

Upon the deadline, the Governing Board Secretary shall verify the vote record and confirm whether the requirements of this policy were met.

The Secretary’s verification shall include:

- Confirmation that an approved electronic voting method was used
- Confirmation that all current Governing Board members were included in the original vote notice
- Confirmation that the Governing Board Secretary was included in the original vote notice and had access to the vote responses and official vote record
- Confirmation that the Chair or Vice-Chair properly initiated the vote
- Confirmation that the voting deadline was followed
- Confirmation that any required follow-up attempts were documented
- The final vote count, including Yes, No, Abstain, and Non-Response
- Whether quorum was achieved
- Whether the required number of Yes votes was achieved
- Whether any objection to electronic consideration was made
- Whether the motion passed or failed under this policy

After the Governing Board Secretary verifies the record, the Chair or Vice-Chair shall announce the result to all Governing Board members.

The announcement shall include:

- The text of the motion that was voted on
- Confirmation that all current Governing Board members were included in the original vote notice
- The final vote count, including Yes, No, Abstain, and Non-Response
- Whether quorum was achieved
- Whether the required number of Yes votes was achieved
- Whether the motion passed or failed

If the Governing Board Secretary determines that the requirements of this policy were not met, the vote shall not be announced as official board action.

If the requirements of this policy were not met, the matter shall either be restarted under this policy or brought to the next regular or properly called meeting of the Governing Board.

If quorum is not achieved by the stated deadline, the vote fails and the matter must be brought to the next regular or properly called meeting, unless the Chair or Vice-Chair determines that the vote should be restarted with a new deadline.

If the required number of Yes votes is not achieved by the stated deadline, the motion fails.

---

## Section 14 - Record Keeping

The Governing Board Secretary shall retain complete documentation of each electronic vote, including:

- The original communication initiating the vote
- The electronic voting method or platform used
- The full recipient list, participant list, or access list for the original vote notice
- The full text of the motion
- Any relevant background or context included with the motion
- Any summary of prior board discussion provided with the motion
- The reason the matter was handled electronically
- The voting deadline
- All individual vote responses received, including the name of each respondent, their vote, and the date and time received, when available
- Any abstentions
- Any non-responses
- Any objection to electronic consideration
- Any commentary submitted by board members
- Any clarification or material revision made during the voting period
- Any follow-up attempts made to non-responding board members
- The Secretary’s verification that the requirements of this policy were or were not met
- The final announcement of the result from the Chair or Vice-Chair

This documentation shall be preserved as part of the official records of the Governing Board and shall be reported at the next regular meeting of the board.

If the platform used for voting does not produce a permanent record suitable for retention, the Governing Board Secretary shall export, save, screenshot, print, or otherwise preserve the record in a durable format before the result is finalized.

---

## Section 15 - Reporting at the Next Meeting

At the next regular meeting of the Governing Board following any electronic vote, the Chair, Vice-Chair, or Governing Board Secretary shall formally report the vote for inclusion in the meeting minutes.

The report shall include:

- The motion
- The reason the vote was conducted electronically
- The electronic voting method used
- The vote count
- Whether quorum was achieved
- Whether the required number of Yes votes was achieved
- Whether the motion passed or failed
- Any objection that suspended or canceled the electronic vote
- Confirmation from the Governing Board Secretary that the requirements of this policy were met

The motion, vote count, and outcome shall be entered into the minutes as an official action of the Governing Board.

If an electronic vote was attempted but did not result in official board action because the requirements of this policy were not met, that fact may also be reported for the record.

---

## Section 16 - Limitations

The following matters may not be decided by electronic vote under this policy and require action at a regular or properly called meeting of the Governing Board:

- Amendments to the bylaws
- Election or removal of officers
- Personnel decisions involving the hiring or termination of staff
- Matters involving church discipline
- Matters involving confidential pastoral care concerns
- Any matter requiring a congregational vote
- Any matter involving significant financial commitments outside the approved budget, unless previously discussed and specifically authorized by the Governing Board
- Any major strategic decision that would materially affect the direction of the church
- Any controversial matter that requires extended discussion or discernment
- Any matter in which Robert’s Rules of Order, Newly Revised requires action at a formal meeting and for which no special authorization has been granted

---

## Section 17 - Consistency with Robert’s Rules of Order

This policy is intended to operate in consistency with Robert’s Rules of Order, Newly Revised, as the parliamentary authority of Riverwood Community Church.

In any matter not addressed by this policy, the applicable provisions of Robert’s Rules of Order and the bylaws of Riverwood Community Church shall govern.
