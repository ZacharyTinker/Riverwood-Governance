# Kitchen & Hospitality — Ministry Roles & Responsibilities

**Riverwood Community Church**  
**Status:** Working draft — not adopted  
**Last updated:** November 20, 2026  
**Approval record:** Not yet approved

## Purpose

The Kitchen & Hospitality Ministry helps create welcoming opportunities for fellowship through food, meals and church gatherings. It organizes kitchen volunteers, supports food preparation and service, and helps keep food service areas clean and orderly.

The Kitchen Team supports other ministries when food service is needed. The ministry organizing an event remains responsible for the overall event.

## Leadership and coordination

- **Kitchen Team Lead → Head Deacon / Head Deaconess**, under Congregational Care oversight (agreed working structure; confirm against approved governance before adoption).
- **Kitchen Crew Members → Kitchen Team Lead.**
- **Sunday School Coffee Volunteer and Sunday School Donut Volunteer → Kitchen Team Lead.** Their descriptions and assignment schedules are maintained in the **Sunday Services / Sunday School Hospitality** section, not duplicated here. They do **not** report to the Sunday Service Coordinator.
- **Kitchen Supplies & Inventory Coordinator → Head Deacon / Head Deaconess**, and works with the Kitchen Team Lead. This assignment is documented under Congregational Care.
- For an event, the Kitchen Team Lead coordinates food service with the event ministry's leader; that leader remains responsible for the whole event.

Where a role is documented does not always determine its reporting line.

# Ministry Roles

## 1. Kitchen Team Lead

**Role Type:** Team lead  
**Serving Classification:** Ministry leadership  
**Accountability:** Head Deacon / Head Deaconess (Congregational Care oversight)

### Purpose
Lead kitchen volunteers and coordinate food service for church meals, fellowship and ministry events.

### Responsibilities
- Organize and schedule Kitchen Crew Members for relevant gatherings.
- Coordinate kitchen setup, food preparation, serving and cleanup.
- Work with event leaders to identify needs, staffing, equipment and preparation times.
- Support volunteers, including helping new volunteers learn kitchen procedures.
- Coordinate food purchases, donations and supplies within approved event plans and financial arrangements.
- Work with the Kitchen Supplies & Inventory Coordinator on needed supplies.
- Maintain appropriate food handling, cleanliness and safety practices during activities.
- Report broken equipment, maintenance concerns and shortages to the appropriate leader.
- **Supervise the Sunday School Coffee and Donut Volunteer assignments**, including coverage, preparation expectations and practical supply needs, while the roles remain documented under Sunday Services.

### Decision-Making Authority
May assign volunteers, organize kitchen work, and make routine food service decisions within approved plans. Significant expenses, equipment purchases and commitments require appropriate approval.

### Service Commitment
Ongoing coordination as needed; greater involvement during meals, events and scheduled activities.

## 2. Kitchen Crew Member

**Role Type:** Volunteer  
**Serving Classification:** General volunteer  
**Accountability:** Kitchen Team Lead

### Purpose
Help prepare, serve and clean up food and refreshments at church activities.

### Responsibilities
- Assist with food and beverage preparation as assigned.
- Help set up serving spaces, tables and equipment.
- Serve guests and assist during meals.
- Clean dishes, equipment, counters and food service areas after use.
- Store supplies appropriately and help keep the kitchen organized.
- Follow food handling and cleaning practices.
- Report supply shortages, broken equipment or concerns to the Kitchen Team Lead.
- Assist with events when available.

### Serving Guidelines
Prior kitchen experience is not required for general volunteer tasks. Duties should match each volunteer's ability, comfort and any applicable safety requirements.

### Service Commitment
Flexible according to events and volunteer availability.

# Kitchen & Hospitality Responsibility Areas

These are **functions**, not additional church offices or job titles.

## 3. Meal Preparation & Food Service

**Purpose:** Help provide meals and refreshments for church gatherings.

**Typical responsibilities:** Coordinate preparation, assign tasks, prepare food and beverages, set up orderly serving areas, follow appropriate food handling practices, serve attendees and clean up afterward.

## 4. Event Kitchen Support

**Purpose:** Support other ministries' events that require food service.

**Typical responsibilities:** Coordinate food plans with the event leader; determine volunteers, equipment and preparation time; help with food donations and purchases; prepare, serve and clean up; report limitations and needs.

**Boundary:** The Kitchen Team oversees the food service portion. The event ministry manages the event's wider schedule, registration, promotion and programming. A new Kitchen role is not needed for each event.

## 5. Kitchen Setup, Cleaning & Organization

**Purpose:** Maintain clean and ready food service spaces.

**Typical responsibilities:** Prepare counters and equipment, maintain clean preparation areas, wash and store dishes and utensils, organize storage areas, remove food-service trash, and report equipment or sanitation concerns.

**Boundary:** Cleaning after kitchen activities does not replace the **paid Custodian's separate employment responsibilities**.

## 6. Food Donations & Contributions

**Purpose:** Help coordinate donated food for meals and gatherings.

**Typical responsibilities:** Communicate event food needs, organize contributions and drop-offs, pay attention to ingredients and dietary needs, use appropriate food storage and handling, and communicate shortages.

**Boundary:** People may contribute food without joining the Kitchen Crew. Food safety expectations still apply.

# Ministry Cooperation

## Congregational Care
The Kitchen Team Lead operates under the Head Deacon / Head Deaconess in the working structure. The Kitchen Supplies & Inventory Coordinator monitors supplies and works closely with the Kitchen Team Lead. The Meal Train Coordinator retains responsibility for meals delivered to families or individuals, though Kitchen Crew Members may help when available.

## Sunday Services and Sunday School Hospitality
The **Coffee Volunteer and Donut Volunteer** assignments are documented under **Sunday Services / Sunday School Hospitality** because they support Sunday School fellowship. Both report to the **Kitchen Team Lead**, not the Sunday Service Coordinator. Coffee is brewed and donuts are brought and made available before **9:30 a.m. Sunday School**. The Kitchen Team may coordinate supplies and volunteer coverage without moving these descriptions into this ministry.

## Community Outreach
The Kitchen Team may support food service at outreach events, while the Community Outreach Director or event coordinator remains responsible for the event as a whole.

## Children & Youth Ministries
Kitchen volunteers may assist with meals for these programs; ministry leaders remain responsible for programming and supervision of minors.

## Trustees & Facilities
Report kitchen equipment and physical maintenance concerns to the Head Trustee or designated Trustee. Trustees coordinate facility repairs; Kitchen Team Members care for equipment during food service.

# Shared Serving Expectations

## Hospitality
Treat attendees with warmth, patience and respect and help create welcoming gatherings.

## Food safety
Use appropriate food handling, storage and cleanliness practices. Do not prepare or serve food when doing so creates an avoidable sanitation concern. Take food allergies and dietary restrictions seriously and do not promise allergen safety without reliable information. Specific training and procedures belong in separate operating guidance.

## Equipment and supplies
Use kitchen equipment responsibly, return items to their places and report damage or shortages.

## Volunteer participation
Occasional volunteers are welcome. Church-wide membership, screening and leadership requirements are governed by centralized serving standards rather than repeated here.

## Financial stewardship
Food and event purchases must follow approved budgets and purchasing authority. Volunteers should not commit church funds without authorization.

# Review and Approval

This draft documents the agreed working reporting arrangement and ministry responsibilities. It does not establish a new elected church office, alter adopted bylaws, or independently grant purchasing authority.
