# Security & Safety — Ministry Roles & Responsibilities

**Riverwood Community Church**  
**Status:** Working draft — not adopted  
**Last updated:** November 20, 2026  
**Approval record:** Not yet approved

## Purpose

The Security & Safety Ministry helps provide a safe, welcoming setting for people participating in Riverwood's worship services, ministries and events. It helps prevent avoidable problems, identifies concerns, supports appropriate emergency responses and assists church leaders when safety needs arise.

**Safety and hospitality work together.** The ministry is intended to help people feel safe and welcome, not create an unnecessarily intimidating atmosphere.

## Leadership and coordination

The **Security Director / Coordinator** leads the Security Team and reports to the **Head Deacon or Head Deaconess through Care Team oversight**. Security Team Members report to the Security Director / Coordinator.

During Sunday services, the Security Team coordinates with the **Sunday Service Coordinator** on service-related operations, while the Security Director / Coordinator retains responsibility for assigned safety activities. The Security Director / Coordinator may communicate urgent safety matters directly to the Lead Pastor and appropriate emergency services.

**Governance note:** This is the agreed working structure and aligns with the proposed 2026 bylaw placement of Security under Care Team oversight. The 2026 amendments are not adopted; this draft itself does not change the adopted bylaws or create an elected office.

### Transition from the current Usher arrangement

Riverwood currently uses Ushers for part of its Sunday safety coverage. Generally, two Ushers remain outside the sanctuary during worship: one near the entrance and one moving throughout the building. They may switch positions as needed.

**The transition to a dedicated Security Team will be gradual.** Existing Usher coverage continues unless and until a specific responsibility is formally reassigned, volunteers are prepared for the change and affected team leaders are informed. The church should avoid gaps or ambiguity about which team is covering an assigned duty. The separate Sunday Services role description remains the source for the Usher role.

# Ministry Roles

## 1. Security Director / Coordinator

**Role Type:** Ministry Director / Coordinator  
**Serving Classification:** Ministry leadership  
**Accountability:** Head Deacon / Head Deaconess (Care Team oversight)

### Purpose

Lead Riverwood's security and safety efforts by coordinating volunteers, appropriate preparation and responses to concerns while maintaining a welcoming environment.

### Responsibilities
- Lead, recruit, organize, schedule and support Security Team volunteers.
- Coordinate appropriate safety coverage for Sunday services and other church activities.
- Help establish and maintain consistent church safety practices under approved policies.
- Ensure volunteers understand their responsibilities, boundaries and reporting processes.
- Coordinate suitable training and emergency preparedness activities.
- Identify and communicate safety concerns and recommendations to church leadership.
- Work with the Sunday Service Coordinator, relevant ministry leaders and Head Trustee on safety needs that affect services, events or facilities.
- Support the development and review of separate emergency response procedures.
- Coordinate appropriate incident documentation under church procedures.
- Guide a **phased handoff** of relevant safety responsibilities from Ushers, while maintaining existing assignments until changes are actually implemented.
- Inform the Lead Pastor and appropriate leaders of significant safety concerns or incidents.

### Decision-Making Authority

May schedule and direct Security Team volunteers, manage routine coverage and coordinate immediate responses consistent with training and approved church procedures. Significant policy changes, major purchases and decisions outside assigned authority require the appropriate approvals.

### Service Commitment

Ongoing preparation, volunteer coordination, scheduling, training and responsiveness to significant safety concerns.

## 2. Security Team Member

**Role Type:** Volunteer  
**Serving Classification:** Safety volunteer  
**Accountability:** Security Director / Coordinator

### Purpose

Help provide a safe and welcoming environment by being attentive, assisting people and responding appropriately to concerns.

### Responsibilities
- Serve during assigned services, events and activities.
- Remain attentive to general safety needs while fulfilling assigned duties.
- Identify and communicate concerns promptly.
- Assist people who need help or directions.
- Follow established church safety and emergency procedures.
- Support appropriate emergency responses and request professional help when needed.
- Participate in assigned training and preparation.
- Treat people with courtesy, patience and discretion.
- Avoid unnecessary confrontation and ask for assistance when situations exceed personal training or authority.
- Protect sensitive information learned while serving.

### Decision-Making Authority

May take reasonable immediate steps within training and approved procedures to obtain help or protect people from imminent harm. Serving on the Security Team does **not** grant law-enforcement powers.

### Serving Guidelines

Dependability, attentiveness, sound judgment and a willingness to follow church procedures are important. Training and screening requirements should match the actual assignment and be established in central church-wide standards.

### Service Commitment

Scheduled services, events and applicable training.

# Safety Responsibility Areas

These are **assignments**, not additional offices or leadership positions.

## 3. Sunday Service Safety Coverage

**Purpose:** Support a safe, welcoming environment during worship services.

**Responsibilities**
- Fulfill assigned coverage responsibilities during services.
- Remain attentive to concerns affecting people and the facility.
- Communicate with the Security Director / Coordinator and Sunday Service Coordinator as needed.
- Assist individuals seeking help or directions.
- Support established emergency procedures and report concerns through approved channels.

**Current arrangement:** Ushers continue their existing entrance and building coverage. Responsibilities shift to Security Team Members only through a coordinated, gradual transition.

## 4. Special Event Safety Coverage

**Purpose:** Support safety at church events outside regular Sunday worship.

**Responsibilities**
- Coordinate with event leaders on general safety needs.
- Provide assigned volunteer coverage where appropriate.
- Identify and report concerns affecting attendees, activities or facilities.
- Assist people and support approved emergency procedures when needed.

**Commitment:** As appropriate to individual events.

## 5. Emergency Preparedness Support

**Purpose:** Help Riverwood prepare for emergencies and support an organized response.

**Responsibilities**
- Participate in relevant training or approved preparedness activities.
- Become familiar with emergency communication and incident-reporting expectations.
- Identify preparation needs and communicate recommendations.
- Assist in approved drills and appropriate responses within training and assigned responsibilities.

**Boundary:** Security volunteers do not replace police, fire, emergency medical personnel or other professional responders. Detailed emergency procedures belong in separate approved documents.

# Working With Other Ministries

## Care Team and Head Deacon / Head Deaconess

The Care Team provides ministry oversight through the Head Deacon or Head Deaconess. The Security Director / Coordinator manages the Security Team's day-to-day work and communicates significant needs, incidents and recommendations to the Head Deacon or Head Deaconess.

## Sunday Services

The Sunday Service Coordinator leads overall Sunday service operations; the Security Director / Coordinator leads safety coverage. They coordinate to avoid unnecessary disruption while ensuring immediate concerns are addressed.

## Ushers and Welcome Team

Ushers currently assist with safety coverage and guest seating/navigation. Their existing responsibilities remain in place during the phased transition. Welcome Team Members assist guests and families and remain under **Sunday Services**, not Care Team oversight. Safety personnel cooperate with these teams without replacing their hospitality responsibilities.

## Children and Youth Ministries

Safety volunteers may assist when a safety concern arises, but children's and youth leaders retain their own program supervision, check-in/release and child-protection responsibilities. A Security Team assignment does not independently authorize supervision of minors.

## Trustees and Facilities

The Security Director / Coordinator reports building-related concerns to the Head Trustee and coordinates on lighting, doors, building access and other physical safety needs. Trustees remain responsible for building maintenance and repairs.

## Lead Pastor and Elders

Pastoral and spiritual decisions remain with appropriate church leadership. Significant matters should be communicated through applicable church channels, including directly to the Lead Pastor when urgent.

# Serving Standards and Procedures

- **Church-wide serving rules:** Applicable membership, volunteer screening, child-protection and leadership requirements belong in Riverwood's central standards; this role document does not duplicate those rules.
- **Emergency procedures:** Detailed instructions for medical incidents, fire, severe weather, missing children and other emergencies belong in approved operating procedures and training materials.
- **Privacy:** Share sensitive incident information only through appropriate reporting channels and protect related records.
- **Equipment:** Specialized safety equipment, permissions and training require separate church approval. This description grants no special equipment-carrying or use authority.
- **Communication:** Each assigned duty should have a clear point of responsibility, especially while Usher coverage is transitioning.
- **Safety policy:** Role descriptions do not independently establish enforcement powers or override church governance and applicable law.

# Review and Approval

This document describes the **proposed** Security & Safety Ministry structure and planned phased transition from Usher coverage. It does not establish new elected offices or alter the adopted bylaws. Current Usher assignments remain active until specific changes are formally implemented.
