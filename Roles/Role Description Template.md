### Editing standard

- Keep the **At a glance** section usable as a volunteer recruitment blurb.
- Use everyday words: “helps organize,” “leads,” “prepares,” “keeps records,” and “reports to.”
- Avoid promising powers, spending limits, voting rights, appointments, or training that have not been confirmed.
- Mark any uncertain detail **Needs confirmation**.
- Keep how-to steps, weekly schedules, and equipment instructions in separate guides.
- Board-approved role boundaries are separate from ordinary task scheduling.
