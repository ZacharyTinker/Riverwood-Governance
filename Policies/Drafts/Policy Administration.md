# Policy Administration

**Riverwood Community Church**  
**Status:** Working draft — not adopted  
**Proposed approval:** Governing Board

## Purpose

The Church Policy Manual explains how Riverwood operates, how responsibilities are assigned, and how church leaders carry out their duties.

The Manual includes separate policies and approved role descriptions. Together, these documents form one official set of church policies.

## Authority

The Governing Board is responsible for adopting, changing, and removing official church policies.

Policies must follow the Church Bylaws, applicable law, and the governing documents of The Christian and Missionary Alliance. A policy cannot change the bylaws or create a church office that the bylaws do not allow.

## Adopting and changing policies

New policies and meaningful changes to existing policies require Governing Board approval at a properly called meeting, using applicable decision-making requirements.

Each adopted policy must include an effective date and a record of the Board action approving it.

An approved policy remains in effect until it is changed or removed. A policy does not expire just because it has not been reviewed recently.

## Role descriptions

Every established leadership, ministry, and volunteer role should have a written description approved at the appropriate level under this policy.

Each description should clearly explain:
- Why the role exists
- What the person is responsible for
- Who provides direction and oversight
- What decisions the person may make
- How the role is filled and any term, if applicable
- Any qualifications, safety checks, or training required
- The typical time commitment, when known

Role descriptions must be consistent with the bylaws and other approved policies.

Directors are generally appointed as described in the bylaws. Coordinators normally serve within a director's ministry area, **but exceptions may apply** where the bylaws, Governing Board, or a separate approved policy establishes a different relationship.

Ministry leaders may assign tasks, organize volunteers, and adjust schedules within the approved purpose of their ministry. A significant change to a role's purpose, responsibilities, or decision-making authority requires Board approval.

## Document maintenance

The Governing Board Secretary keeps the official copies of adopted policies, role descriptions, and records of changes.

The Secretary may correct spelling, formatting, and links without changing an approved policy's meaning. Any substantive revision follows the required approval process.

Working drafts and older versions must be clearly marked so they are not mistaken for current policy.

## Review

The Governing Board may review a policy at any time and should review it when needs, church operations, or governing requirements change.
